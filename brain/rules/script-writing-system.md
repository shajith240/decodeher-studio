# Script writing system (10-17) — how every reel script is written

Owner 10-17: took a second opinion (ChatGPT's playbook, `brain/rules/dating-reels-script-playbook.md`) and asked to refine our
script writing with it. This file is the MERGE: the playbook's method + our owner-approved rules. Where they conflict, the
owner's rules win (marked). The writer (tools/cloud/writer_prompt.txt) follows this file.

## Workflow for every script
1. **One viewer problem, one lesson.** A specific moment a man lives (her exact text or behaviour), and the one thing he
   should understand or do. Every reel is about women/dating (owner rule, niche-only).
2. **Pick a template** (list below). Rotate: never the same template twice in a row; a batch of 6 uses at least 4 different
   templates; our approved "decode + three moves" shape (T0) is used at most twice per batch.
3. **Write three genuinely different hooks** (different angles from playbook §3), then pick the strongest. Hook = her
   specific behaviour + tension, understood on first hearing (brain/research/hooks-2026.md).
4. **Open on the situation.** No greeting, no intro, no "Listen up", no "Here are 3 tips".
5. **Concrete and spoken:** exact texts in quotes, observable actions, plain words (spoken-script-rules.md). Only words a
   person really says out loud (no haha/lol, owner 10-17).
6. **Deliver the payoff the hook promised**, with one real middle example and a usable next step (often the exact text to send).
7. **Length: 30–50 s of natural speech; cut every filler line.** (OWNER RULE wins over the playbook's 20–35 s: no hard cap,
   value first, never rush or squeeze the voice. Shorter is better only when nothing of value is lost.)
8. **Closing line only when it fits:** a real reason to send (a specific friend), save (a checklist), or follow (a named next
   part). Keep the batch's experiment arm from brain/growth/next-batch-plan.md. Never beg.
9. **Check before returning:** facts, ethics, originality, no over-generalising.

## Scorecard (in the draft as notes; revise anything below 4 once)
Hook clarity · specificity · payoff · natural delivery · shareability · factual care · originality, each 1–5.

## Templates (details and examples in the playbook §2)
- **T0 Decode + three moves** (our approved Rebelvids-style shape): hook → "Listen" turn → real psychology idea in plain words
  → three moves → why → closing line. Max 2 per batch.
- **T1 Uncomfortable reframe** — "The problem isn't X. It's Y." → example → better action.
- **T2 Scene, freeze, rewrite** — her line + his bad reply → "Freeze." → why it backfires → the better reply, word for word.
- **T3 Decode the tactic** — name the behaviour (never a diagnosis) → one observable example → one-off vs pattern → boundary line.
- **T4 Text autopsy** — the bad text → the friction point → the rewrite → why it's clearer.
- **T5 Myth, mechanism, move** — popular rule → what it gets wrong → the accurate principle → one move.
- **T6 Three-question self-audit** — a check to run before sending → three questions → how to use the answers (save-bait).
- **T7 Mini-story with a turn** — start at the consequence, two details, the mistaken assumption, the lesson. Told as "you"
  or "say a guy…" — never presented as a real person's true story.
- **T8 Decision tree** — the ambiguous event → two or three explanations → the signal that tells them apart → one calm step.
- **T9 Red flag vs green flag** — one behaviour in two versions → the difference → one observable test.
- **T10 One-sentence rule, then the exception** — memorable rule → example → the exception → the practical test.

## Topic pillars (rotate, playbook §4)
Self-command (overthinking, rejection) · communication (invites, follow-ups) · social awareness (reciprocity) · manipulation
literacy (guilt, hot-and-cold, silent treatment) · attraction myths · standards and walking away · small social skills.
All framed around women and dating.

## Honesty rules (from the playbook; they match our ethics rule)
- No "all women / every woman / always / never" claims; no mind-reading; say "often", "can", give context.
- Real psychology terms only, used correctly, explained in plain words; never diagnose a person (no "she's a narcissist").
- Never invent statistics, studies, sources or personal stories. No "my friend" stories presented as true (replaces the old
  "one real moment to my friend" beat in spoken-script-rules.md: use "you" or an obvious hypothetical).
- Never teach jealousy, silent treatment, pressure, deception or making her insecure as ways to attract. Manipulation is
  explained so he can recognise it and answer with a boundary.
- Never promise a line "guarantees" attraction. Useful even if the viewer never follows.
- Don't copy other creators' wording; borrow structure only. Ban as defaults: "The truth is", "Most men don't know",
  "She will never tell you", "Read that again", "This is your sign".
