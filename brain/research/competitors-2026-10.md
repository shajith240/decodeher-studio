# Competitor research: dating / female-psychology short-form (October 2026)

Collected 2026-10-13 for @thedecodeher. Scope: faceless and face-on dating, "female psychology", "dark psychology for men", texting, attraction, body language, and stick-figure or silhouette explainers.

## How the data was collected (read this first)

- **YouTube (strongest data).** Subscriber counts come from the channel page header and YouTube's channel search. View, like and comment counts come from the public watch pages via yt-dlp, read on 2026-10-13. Opening lines are quoted from YouTube's auto-captions, so the transcription can contain small errors. "Top shorts" means the highest view counts among the channel's latest ~300 Shorts, which is everything the Shorts tab lists.
- **Instagram (partial).** Follower and post counts come from each profile's public `og:description` tag, and bios and highlights from the public profile page. Instagram does not show reel view counts without a login, so **no Instagram reel view count in this file is verified.** Several profiles (psych2go, hoe_math, sadiapsychology1) returned an empty or blocked page and are marked unverified. masculine.doctrine is age-gated (25+).
- **TikTok: not verified.** tiktok.com is blocked in India and returned the government-block notice, so no TikTok follower counts appear here. Where a creator links a TikTok, that is noted. The only TikTok-scale claim used is the VICE article's #darkpsychology hashtag figure.
- Tags: **[V]** = verified, with the source URL in the row or in Sources. **[E]** = estimate or inference, reasoning given. Counts move daily, so treat them as a 2026-10-13 snapshot.
- I left out every account I could not confirm exists. Search turned up dozens of tiny "Female Psychology ___" YouTube channels (100 to 9K subscribers). They are listed only as evidence that the space is crowded with AI-made channels.

## 3. Synthesis

### 3.1 Content patterns that reliably go viral (with evidence)

1. **"Signs she likes you / signs she's not interested."** The most-viewed evergreen topic. Evidence: Psych2Go 14.97M and 11.3M, Beyond Attraction 2.14M, Practical Psychology 6.0M, Courtney Ryan 1.46M, Blaine's "5 signs a girl is playing you" 626K and "Types of eye contact" 489K.
2. **"What women actually notice", in a woman's voice or from women's answers.** Courtney Ryan 4.47M and 4.45M ("Sexiest things men wear according to women", "Masculine habits women find attractive"), Blaine 1.73M. Crowd-sourcing ("I asked the ladies") gives the claim authority without a credential.
3. **Diagnose a real quote or clip.** hoe_math's top three Shorts (8.35M, 5.06M, 4.12M) all react to a woman's real dating-app or TikTok statement. Charisma on Command's 9.78M Short breaks down a talk-show moment. A real artefact plus calm analysis beats a generic list.
4. **Self-respect, disrespect and conflict.** Charisma on Command currently gets 0.6–1.4M per video on this ("Immune To Disrespect", "Respect You During Conflict"), Mindplicit 1.15M ("Never explain yourself"), Dark Needle's "Destroying Her Ego…" 156K and "Brutal Reason She Does Not Respect You". Men respond to dignity, not tricks.
5. **The relatable scene opening ("this is you…" / "there's this girl…").** The Dark Needle's hook rule, copied by Beyond Attraction and PsycheDepth ("Picture this…"). It works on long-form and should transfer to Reels.
6. **Short numbered lists with no preamble.** Blaine's 17–28 s list reels top 1M. Courtney's ~60 s lists top 4M. The title is the first spoken line.
7. **Acted skits / mini-dramas.** Iron Man Lifestyle's top three Shorts in 2026 are all acted situations (1.6M, 1.4M, 1.3M), against 8–46K for its daily advice Shorts. Story beats advice by an order of magnitude in the same account. This is the strongest evidence for our relatable-story direction.
8. **A personal reveal at the end.** The Dark Needle's newest video closes with "the girl I described was someone I knew at 22, I lost a year to her". It turns a lesson into a story and gives viewers a reason to comment.
9. **Series and part numbers.** hoe_math uses "PT2", "PART II", "RED FLAG #8/#9", "LEVELS [1]". Beyond Attraction closes with a next-episode tease. The Dark Needle asks whether to continue the "social skills series".
10. **Grievance and rage bait (works, but we should not use it).** PsycheDepth's "Why Women Over 40 Go Crazy" earned 775K views and 8,900 comments, the highest comment rate measured. Hamza's crude framings earn 2–3.6M. They grow fast and poison the brand. Listed here so we can recognise the pattern, not copy it.

**Lengths that win [V]:** IG-native lists run 17–28 s (Blaine). Shorts explainers and reactions run 43–60 s (hoe_math, Courtney, Charisma). Skits run 33–78 s (Iron Man). Nothing in the top Short set runs past 78 s.

**Like-rate benchmarks [V, likes ÷ views on top Shorts]:** Hamza 7.3% · Charisma 3.7% · hoe_math 3.3% · Courtney 2.7% · Seda 2.1% · Iron Man 1.6–1.9% · Blaine (YT mirror) 1.5%. The Dark Needle reaches 4.2% on long-form.

### 3.3 Mechanics: adopt or avoid

**Adopt (they fit an honest brand):**
- **Scene-first hook in the first 1–2 s** (Dark Needle). We already do this.
- **Real-quote diagnosis** (hoe_math, Charisma): an on-screen speech bubble with a paraphrased real-world line, then the explanation. Paraphrase or use public-domain quotes. Do not screenshot private people.
- **Numbered parts and series names** (hoe_math): for example "Decoded #4" or "Mixed Signals, Part 2". Reply to the best comment with the next part to create a loop.
- **Comment-prompt CTA** that asks for the viewer's own story or answer (Blaine's "List your 3 goals 👇"). This fits our "follow for more" spoken-CTA rule (`cta-rule.md`): end with a question, then "follow for more".
- **Share prompt aimed at a friend** ("send this to the friend who double-texts"). Sends per reach is the strongest reported Reels signal for reaching non-followers ([SocialPilot 2026](https://www.socialpilot.co/es/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm), a marketing source, not primary).
- **Save-worthy list beats** (3–5 items) for the save signal.
- **Highlights as an index, not as social proof.** Competitors use highlights for testimonials and "Results". We sell nothing, so we should use them as a topic index: "Texting", "Signs", "Confidence", "First Date", "Series".
- **Daily cadence where we can sustain it** (Iron Man posts daily). Story-skits outperform, so the volume should be stories.

**Avoid (these conflict with a non-manipulative brand that sells nothing):**
- "Make her obsessed / addicted", "destroy her ego", "control", "weaknesses of women", Machiavelli seduction. These are the Dark Needle's own titles and the dark-psych genre's. The **topic** (why she pulls away, how to stop over-pursuing) is fine. The **promise of control** is not. A VICE critique notes that "dark psychology" is not a scientific concept and has been compared to pickup artistry ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/dark-psychology-tiktok-trend/)).
- Rage bait about groups of women ("women over 40 go crazy", "dumb women", "A.W.F.U.L. women").
- Comment-keyword → DM funnels (ManyChat "comment TEXT"). They exist to sell courses. We could use them to send a free resource, but they add automation risk and look like a sales funnel.
- "Click below" course funnels and Calendly links in every caption.
- Fake crowd-sourcing ("I asked 1,000 women") without actually doing it.
- Copying titles verbatim, as Beyond Attraction and PsycheDepth do with the Dark Needle's. Instagram's 2025-26 push toward original content reportedly cuts aggregator reach. That claim is unverified and comes from secondary sources.

### 3.4 Gaps our silhouette-story style could own

1. **The Dark Needle's look is absent from Reels and Instagram.** The style leader has 481K on YouTube and 910 followers with 1 post on IG, and posts no Shorts at all. Its clones (Beyond Attraction, PsycheDepth, Mindplicit) are also YouTube long-form with no IG presence (Mindplicit IG: 666 followers, 0 posts). **No verified account turns glowing-silhouette relationship stories into 30–60 s Reels.**
2. **Stories over lists.** Iron Man's data shows acted stories beating advice 10–50x, yet faceless pages keep making lists. A faceless page that animates the story (Iron Man-style drama, but silhouettes and no actors) has no competitor I could find.
3. **Respect without contempt.** The two growing clusters are "self-respect / conflict" (Charisma, Mindplicit) and "female psychology" (PsycheDepth). The second is mostly grievance-driven. Nobody combines "understand her" with "respect yourself" without sliding into control or contempt. That is DecodeHer's position.
4. **The "woman explains women" lane is open.** Courtney Ryan (890K) quit, and Blaine is a matchmaker-funnel brand. A faceless page can't use a female face, but it can **openly source** women's real answers (polls, published surveys, research) and dramatise them. That is honest borrowed authority.
5. **Accuracy as a feature.** Every account here states claims without sources. Short on-screen tags ("Study: Hinge 2024", "Body-language research, Navarro") would set us apart and protect us from the "pseudo-science" critique aimed at the genre.
6. **Texting shown, not told.** Texting topics get views ("Master the Art of Texting Women" 769K, Iron Man "Once You Understand Texting…" 182K), but most creators just talk about it. Animated phone-screen silhouettes with message bubbles (a Dark Needle prop) suit a 30 s Reel well.
7. **A playful, non-hostile series format.** hoe_math's "ick indicator" chart reached 8.35M. A recurring visual device, such as a purple "signal meter" that rates mixed signals, could become our signature without his adversarial tone.
