## VIDEO 5: "Steal viral background songs"

### 1. What it is and who it's for
A talking-head creator-education reel for people who make Reels and Shorts. In the hook he promises songs that "go viral
again and again". The middle 26 s is a silent showcase: each song plays over a music-player card. It ends with a comment-keyword
lead magnet. Its only caption is a static title card.

**Tips it teaches (paraphrased):**
1. Pick the background track by the **vibe** of the video, not by what is trending. Its four vibe buckets are **Hype**,
   **Cinematic**, **Suspense** and **Motivation**, with two example tracks in each:
   - Hype: Broke in a Minute, Timeless
   - Cinematic: Dragonfly, Luminary
   - Suspense: "Mysterious and Sad", "Suspense Horror Piano and Music Box"
   - Motivation: Frank Saint (slowed), Way Down We Go
2. Reuse proven tracks. A song that has carried viral videos before is a safe bed for yours.
3. Let the audio sell itself. Each track plays alone for about 2.5–3.5 s with no talking over it.
4. Close with a **comment-a-keyword** offer ("comment VIBES and I'll send the full list of 100, sorted by vibe"). It is a
   lead magnet that also farms comments.

For us, the Suspense and Cinematic buckets fit dark-psychology dating content. Trending commercial songs are only safe through
Instagram's own music library, and business accounts get a limited catalogue. See [[voice-and-assets-licensing]] before using any.

### 2. Caption system
- **There are no voice captions at all.** Only a title card, "STEAL VIRAL / BACKGROUND SONGS". Line 1 is mint green and line 2
  is white. Both are heavy, extended, italic, ALL CAPS, with a dark drop shadow. It sits at chest height (about y 47% of the
  frame) and stays static through the whole intro (0–5.7 s) and again at the end (36.7–40.8 s).
- Words shown vs spoken: 4 words on screen against 2.6 words/s spoken. The title is a label, not a transcript.
- Song cards are centred (about y 30–42%). From top to bottom: a genre pill in small caps with skip icons, a rounded-square
  album art, the **title** in bold white sans, and the artist in small grey.
- **CTA typewriter** (`cr-j.png`, from 31.4 s): a comment-input pill fades in at about 31.53 s. "VIBES" is typed one letter at a
  time: V at 31.73, VI at 31.87, VIB at 31.97, VIBE at 32.07, VIBES at 32.20, so about 0.1 s per letter. That runs about 0.4 s
  **ahead** of the spoken "Vibes" (32.10–32.66). The send button flashes at about 32.4 s.

| Hard cut of album art inside a fixed card frame | Song changes within a genre (8.73 s: "Broke in a Minute" → "Timeless") | 1 frame. The card frame and genre label stay, and only the art, the title and the B-roll behind change. |
| Typewriter | CTA keyword | Steps of about 0.1 s per character |

### 4. Premium signals
- B-roll behind the cards is **desaturated and pushed down to about 25–35% brightness** with a slow drift. The card is the
  only bright object, so the eye has nowhere else to go.
- One consistent UI system (pill, card, notification) makes it look like a product, not an edit.
- Depth: UI layers sit behind the presenter, so the scene has three planes.
- Sound: the songs play at full level (about −24 dB mean) with no voice on top, so the music sells itself. The last ~2.5 s
  are digital silence (−91 dB), which works as a loop point.

## VIDEO 6: "I don't have time" (Islamic prayer reminder)

### 1. What it is and who it's for
A faith-reminder reel for young Muslims. It has a warm amber talking head (static medium close-up, shallow depth of field)
cut against cinematic stills (bedroom, mosque, alarm clock, cemetery) and two in-palette infographics. **It teaches no
editing tips out loud.** It is a style reference, and its tips live in its structure.

**Tips its structure shows (paraphrased):**
1. **Open by quoting the viewer's own excuse.** "I don't have time." (0–0.9 s) is the line the target viewer says, then the
   video questions it right away (1.3 s).
2. **Compare with something everyday.** We drag ourselves to work and put up with people we don't like for comfort, so why not
   this?
3. **Make the abstract countable.** "All five prayers ≈ one hour" is shown as an icon grid. "250,000 people die every day"
   is shown as a running counter and a line chart.
4. **Ask a rhetorical question at each turn** ("Is one hour really too much…?", "You think you still have time?").
5. **Turn hard at the end.** "But death doesn't wait." The video ends on a heavy line and holds it (49.4 s → end).
6. **Censor charged words** ("de\*th") so the platform doesn't suppress the reel. The word stays readable.

### 2. Caption system
**Fonts (two tiers, three faces):**
- Small line: a heavy **rounded geometric sans**, sentence case, **not italic**, white with a soft drop shadow. About 30–36 px
  on a 720-wide frame, or 4–5% of the width.
- Key word, style A: the same heavy sans, about 2.2–2.5× bigger, filled with an **orange-to-gold vertical gradient**. Used for
  blunt, concrete words: *tired, like, many, excuse, called, de\*th, time*.
- Key word, style B: a high-contrast **serif italic** in the same gold gradient. Used for abstract, emotional or spiritual
  words: *deal with, comfortable, final Adhan, true purpose, Hereafter, eternal Paradise, an hour, guests*.
- Only one accent colour (amber/gold), and it matches the warm lamps in every shot.

**Position:** on the talking head, centre-lower over the chest (about y 55–65%), with the face never covered. On B-roll it moves
to the empty part of the frame (the top for "excuse", the bottom for "But de\*th doesn't wait").

**Grouping and timing**, measured at 5 fps over 9–17 s (`cr-p.png`) plus the 30/60 fps strips:

| Group | Words | Visible from–to | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| We get **tired** | 3 | 9.17–9.60 | 0.45 s |
| during the day | 3 | 9.73–10.2 | 0.45 s |
| and sometimes | 2 | 10.4–10.9 | 0.5 s |
| have to **deal with** people | 5 | 11.0–11.9 | 0.9 s |
| we don't **like** | 3 | 12.0–12.75 | 0.75 s |
| We deal with **many** things | 5 | 12.85–13.9 | 1.05 s |
| we don't want to | 4 | 14.0–14.5 | 0.55 s |
| for a more **comfortable** life. | 5 | 14.6–16.2 (cut) | 1.6 s |
| Yet, all five prayers | 4 | 19.43–20.9 | about 1.5 s |
| only take about **an hour.** | 5 | 20.9–22.3 (cut) | about 1.4 s |
| But **de\*th** doesn't wait. | 4 | 36.03–37.7 | about 1.7 s |
| the **final Adhan** | 3 | 47.73–48.73 | 1.0 s |
| has already been **called.** | 4 | 48.83–50.3 (end) | 1.5 s+ |

- **Words per group: 2–5, typically 3–4. Exactly one key word per group** (or one two-word key phrase).
- **On screen per group: 0.45–1.7 s, median about 0.9 s.** Mid-sentence fragments are short (about 0.5 s). The **group that
  ends a sentence is held until the cut**: 0.15–0.6 s past its last word, through the voice's pause.
- **Builds word by word.** Every spoken word appears, so words shown = words spoken. The rate is **about 4.1 words/s during
  speech** and **3.6 words/s overall**, so this voice is NOT slow. It reads comfortably because of the **pauses between
  sentences**: there is a 0.25–0.6 s gap at every sentence end (0.88→1.30, 8.68→9.16, 35.66→36.04, 44.46→45.06).
- **Anchor words held for the whole shot.** On B-roll, one theme word is set huge (about 180–200 px at 720 wide, roughly
  the frame width), sits **behind the subject** (the person is cut out in front of it), and **stays for the whole shot**
  while the small lines cycle under it:
  - "excuse" from 1.3 to 3.3 s (2.0 s)
  - "Salah" from 16.2 to 19.4 s (3.2 s)
  - "1 hour" behind the presenter with a clock from 22.3 to 25.1 s (2.8 s)
  - "like" behind "we don't" from 12.2 to 12.75 s

  **This is the main readability device.** The meaning-word stays long even when the fragments move fast.
- **Text slightly leads the voice.** Text time vs Whisper word onset:
  - "We" 9.17 vs 9.16; "get" 9.27 vs 9.28; "tired" 9.37 vs 9.44; "day" 9.93 vs 10.10
  - "the" 47.73 vs 47.82; "has" 48.85 vs 49.06; "don't" (bubble) 18.47 vs 18.54; "I" (bubble) 18.30 vs 18.18

  Each word appears **0–170 ms before** it is spoken (median about 70 ms early) and **never more than about 120 ms after**.
  Our rule ([[rules/captions]] #4, "30 ms after") is the opposite.
- **Cuts come just before the sentence.** Shot cuts land **80–140 ms before** the first word of the next sentence
  (9.08/9.16, 12.83/12.86, 16.20/16.28, 19.42/19.52, 35.90/36.04, 41.64/41.78). The new picture registers, then the first
  caption word arrives with the voice.

### 4. Premium signals (v6)
- **One grade everywhere.** Amber/tungsten key, brown-black shadows, shallow depth of field. The infographics use the same
  brown gradient with a faint dot grid, so nothing looks pasted in. The caption gold is the scene's lamp colour.
- **Hierarchy through typeface, not effects.** A plain sans line plus one big gradient key word, with a serif italic for
  feeling-words. There are no outlines, no boxes and no glow.
- **Air.** Each sentence ends with a 0.25–0.6 s voice pause, the final group is held through it, and short empty-frame beats
  sit between groups. The screen is never full: at most about 5 words plus one anchor word.
- **Negative space.** On the talking head, text only ever sits in the chest band. On B-roll, text goes into the empty sky,
  floor or wall, and the anchor word sits behind the subject for depth.
- **Pacing curve.** Fast cuts in the hook (about 1.1 s apart for 0–10 s), then a calm 2.5–3.5 s, then data scenes, then the
  hard turn. The talking head returns as the "home base" between B-roll ideas.
- **Camera.** Locked off. In the cemetery shot the 1.5 s sample (36.0–37.5) looks static; I saw no push-in, so the polish
  comes from grade and type, not camera moves.
- **Sound.** A quiet bed sits about 17 dB under the voice (gaps at −38 to −41 dB vs speech at −22 dB). No audible whoosh/SFX
  stacking.

## 5. Recommendations for OUR reels (black void, white silhouettes with purple rim, Montserrat Black Italic)

### A. Animation vocabulary to add (GSAP)
Use these in place of the one slide-from-right. Every word gets an entrance chosen by its role.

| Role of the word / moment | Entrance | GSAP |
|---|---|---|
| **Default small-line word** (most words; "why would she…") | Rise + unblur | `from(w,{opacity:0, y:22, filter:'blur(6px)', duration:0.22, ease:'power3.out'})` |
| **Key word** (the purple line, e.g. "date") | Settle from a dim ghost | `fromTo(w,{opacity:0.25, y:30, scale:1.04, filter:'blur(8px)', color:'#4a2a6b'},{opacity:1, y:0, scale:1, filter:'blur(0px)', color:'#B26BFF', duration:0.35, ease:'expo.out'})`. No overshoot. |
| **Anchor / theme word** for a scene ("GHOSTED", "DATE", "AVOIDANT") | Huge purple word **behind the silhouette** at low opacity, held for the whole beat | `fromTo(a,{opacity:0, scale:1.08},{opacity:0.3, scale:1, duration:0.6, ease:'sine.out'})`, then hold until the scene cut and hard-clear with the cut. Z-order: behind the figure, above the void. |
| **Question** (one built by words, ending in "?") | Default rise per word. On the "?" word only, add a 0.1 s later and slightly bigger settle. **Hold the full question ≥ 0.8 s after the last word.** | as the default, plus `"+=0.1"` on the last word |
| **Number / stat** | Count-up + optional line draw | `gsap.to(obj,{val:target, duration:1.2, ease:'power3.out', snap:{val:1}, onUpdate:render})`. Draw the axes first (0.3 s, `power2.inOut`, stroke-dashoffset). |
| **List items** (red flags 1-2-3) | Staggered pop | `from(items,{opacity:0, scale:0.7, duration:0.22, ease:'back.out(1.4)', stagger:0.27})`, timed to the beat |
| **Inner thought / quote** | Bubble (WhatsApp bubble or thought cloud) grows from the speaker, **holds empty for about 0.3 s**, then words come in per word | `from(bubble,{scale:0.2, opacity:0, transformOrigin:'tail', duration:0.22, ease:'back.out(1.3)'})` |
| **CTA keyword** ("comment X") | Typewriter in a comment pill, then morph to a DM notification | `steps` at 0.09 s per char. The pill morphs into the notification over 0.3 s, `power2.inOut`. |
| **Punchline** (last line) | Default build, then **hold to the end** with nothing else moving | — |
| **Exit of a group** | Hard clear (1 frame) or `to(g,{opacity:0, duration:0.12, ease:'power1.in'})`. Never slide out. | — |
| **Charged words** | Censor with an asterisk in the caption (e.g. "s\*x", "ch\*ating" if needed) | — |

Keep, unchanged: the single accent colour, no glow, and the owner's rejection of flicker and bounce.

### B. Readability fix (complaint a)
The reference does **not** slow the words down (about 4 words/s). It makes them easy to read in three ways:
1. **Max 4 words per caption group** (5 only if one is "a/the/to"). **One key word per group.**
2. **Minimum on-screen time 0.7 s per group.** If a phrase is shorter, merge it with the next fragment. The **group that ends
   a sentence holds until the next sentence starts**, usually 0.3–0.6 s past its last word.
3. **Put 0.35–0.5 s of silence between sentences in the TTS script** (break tags or separate generations). The reference has
   a pause at every sentence end, and that pause is the reading time.
4. **Build word by word** (keep it), but make the entrance 0.22 s with a rise and no sideways travel. The eye stays on one spot.
5. **Use one anchor word per scene**, held for 2–3.5 s behind the figures, so the scene's meaning stays readable even while
   the small lines change.
6. **Timing (proposed rule change):** show each word **50 ms before** its measured onset, not 30 ms after. The reference leads
   by 0–170 ms, and a word that lands as it is heard reads as on-beat. Do **not** run ahead of the voice by more than 0.15 s.
7. **Cut 0.1 s before the sentence's first word**, so the picture change registers before the caption starts.

### C. Script and structure tips to adopt
- **Hook with the viewer's own sentence** in quotes ("He's just busy.", "I don't need closure."), then question it in the
  next line.
- **One everyday comparison** in the middle (the work/school-commute move).
- **Make one claim countable** per reel: a number with a counter, or a small list with staggered items.
- **A rhetorical question at each turn**, and a hard "But…" turn before the CTA.
- **Music by vibe.** Use Suspense or Cinematic beds for our niche, at about −17 dB under the voice, with no SFX over words.
- **Comment-keyword CTA** as an option alongside "follow for more" ([[cta-rule]]): "Comment READ and I'll send you…".
  It needs a real DM automation or deliverable first.

### Proposed rules (for the main agent to merge)
1. `rules/captions.md` #4: change "30 ms after" to "50 ms before the measured onset, never > 150 ms early". Change the entrance
   to "rise 22 px + blur 6→0, 0.22 s power3.out". Remove slide-from-right as the default.
2. Captions: max 4 words per group, one key word, min 0.7 s on screen, and a sentence-final group holds to the next sentence.
3. Audio/TTS: 0.35–0.5 s pause at every sentence end.
4. Visuals: one **anchor word** per scene, behind the figures at 30% opacity, faded in (no flicker), held until the cut.
5. Role-based entrances per the table in §5A (question / number / list / quote / punchline).
