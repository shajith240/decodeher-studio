## 1. Shot grammar
- Text beats change every 1-2 s in the hook (aAW 0:08-0:10, EhT 0:07-0:08). Mid-video builds hold about 4-8 s (sampled sheets).
- Figures: 1-2 on screen in most beats (aAW 0:04-0:06, NSq 0:08-0:12). A crowd appears only as a set piece (NSq circle of seated figures, sampled ~1:30-2:00).
- Focus by glow: the speaking or key figure is bright, others are absent or dim (aAW 0:01).
- Motion: figures walk in from frame edge and out again; camera pushes are rare; most "camera" is a line or arrow drawn across a fixed frame (aAW 0:04-0:05, NSq 0:04-0:05).

## 2. Recurring visual devices
- Host icon: a round head with a vertical needle, used as the narrator silhouette (aAW 5:27 pointing, NSq 0:09). Sets the "Dark Needle" brand.
- Gaze/arrow lines: a thin white line from one figure to another when she looks or he approaches (aAW 0:04-0:05). Dashed coloured arrows show messages travelling (NSq 0:04 red, 0:08 blue).
- Speech bubbles: short quoted lines in a rounded box (NSq 0:08 "ABSOLUTELY. I'LL BE READY."; lW3 0:12 "WANNA GO OUT TONIGHT?" / "SORRY BABE, I'M BUSY" in a mid-video red-mountain diagram).
- Labels stacked above a figure (aAW 0:01-0:02 "STUPID / AVERAGE GUY"; EhT 0:06-0:08 "MONEY / JOB / FACE / HOW YOU TREAT PEOPLE").
- Highlight colours: red for the bad signal (NSq "EXCEPT YOU", lW3 "WHOEVER CHASES, LOSES" in red), yellow for the key term (lW3 "WHY DOES SHE STILL TALK TO ME?"), green/orange for groups (NSq 2:00-2:30).
- Single words on black as a beat marker: "REMEMBER", "UNDERSTAND" (lW3 sampled 1:30-2:00); "COMPLETE GUIDE ON HOW TO START" (aAW ~0:56).
- Props: phone with lock screen 14:49 (NSq 0:01-0:07), clipboard (lW3 ~1:20-1:40 "ASK HER OUT"), spotlight (lW3 ~1:50).

## 3. Text
- Bold sans-serif all caps, white, with a yellow or red accent word. Scales large in mid-video diagrams and small (~1/30 of frame height) in the hook.
- Appears word by word, line by line, in sync with the voice (aAW 0:08-0:11 "WHAT AM I GOING TO SAY TO HER FIRST?" builds as narrator says it).
- Holds 1-4 s, then clears or is replaced by the next line.
- Text is placed at frame edges at times, so it clips on a 16:9 frame (EhT 0:11 "HOW YOU TI..."). Vertical needs its own layout.

## 6. Rules to copy for 30-45 s vertical Reels
1. Open on a glowing figure or a white line of text on black within 1 s.
2. Show the spoken key phrase as text when it is said (aAW 0:08-0:10).
3. Keep one or two figures per beat; one figure, one idea.
4. Use glow to focus: the speaking figure is lit, others dim or absent.
5. One visual metaphor per beat (phone with arrow, lineup, clock, spotlight).
6. Animate in place (draw, wipe, fade) instead of many hard cuts.
7. Labels appear with the word they name and hold 1-3 s.
8. One accent colour (red = bad, yellow = key term) on one word per line.
9. Use a single narrator icon (needle-head silhouette) that recurs.
10. Put music under the voice and let it drop on key lines.

## 5 things that will not translate to Reels
1. The 11-13 minute length: two-part structure (theory, then practice) at aAW 1:06.
2. Long talking holds where one figure stays and narration runs 20+ s.
3. Multi-line stacks and charts (EhT 0:08 four labels; aAW 5:00 bracelet diagram) become unreadable at phone size.
4. 16:9 layouts that clip labels at frame edges; Reels need 9:16 layouts.
5. Mixed visual families within one video (cartoon, silhouette, graph); a 30-45 s Reel should stick to one.
