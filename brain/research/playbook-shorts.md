# Playbook: faceless psychology Shorts (research)

**Scope and limits.** Search (yt-dlp, about 375 results across 15 queries) returned very few psychology Shorts under 70 s with >1k views. Seven faceless videos were watched (30 frames each, plus captions/audio analysis). Three high-view talking-head videos (6sTL6wm_hl8, B8u97x5Ggs4, fx__KIneJ-o) were excluded from the table and used only for caption notes. **None of the sample uses black backgrounds or glowing silhouettes**, so that style is not validated by this data. Views are search-snapshot values. Most sample audio is Hindi.

## 2. Hook formulas (exact examples)

- **Numbered list announce:** "5 Psychology Facts About Human" (fO9Q); "10 Mind Blowing Psychology Facts" (tXao).
- **Ordinal fact:** "Fact Number 1, According to psychology..." (E47).
- **Research authority:** "Research shows that..." (Qvoov); "according to research..." (fO9Q, lB2L).
- **Viewer-mirror "you" claim:** "Being unable to get someone off your mind indicates that you are also on that person's mind" (NjaS).
- **Promise of gain:** "...which lets you impress anyone" (NFyB).

## 3. Pacing

- Scene-change score >0.25 (ffmpeg, flashes included): stock/flash templates cut every 0.3-0.7 s (fO9Q 0.34 s, lB2L 0.34 s, NFyB 0.31 s, tXao 0.66 s). E47 about 1.1 s. NjaS about 3.1 s.
- Numbered format: 10 numerals in 60 s is about one card every 6 s (tXao). fO9Q's "5" appears at 0:00.
- Text: header persists across the whole video (fO9Q, lB2L). Captions change every 1-3 s.
- Endings are CTA, not loops. fO9Q's voiceover ends "subscribe the channel and like" (~0:58). B8u97x5Ggs4's final 5 s is a red subscribe/bell card.

## 4. Caption patterns

- **Header bar:** fO9Q and lB2L colour-code each word in one bar: green, yellow, red, white. Our purple highlight should be one word per card, not per word.
- **Centre caption:** NFyB shows 1-3 words, white, bold, with glow, mid-screen.
- **Card captions:** NjaS shows 12-18 words, centred on a dark translucent box, 3-4 lines. B8u97x5Ggs4 uses a cream card with a 3-6-word sentence and a bold header.
- **Animation:** no animated word-in effects were detectable in frames. Captions cut rather than animate.

## 5. Explanation devices (timestamps)

- Numerals as chapter markers: "1" near 0:02, "3" at 0:36 (lB2L, neon numeral), tXao numeral per fact.
- Icon with label: head-outline icon with "PSYCHOLOGY" (lB2L, about 0:18).
- Stock illustration of the fact (fO9QQGi7KiY, watch at about 0:04).
- Glowing light-leak transitions between clips (NFyB, repeated across the video).
- Not found in the seven faceless videos: arrows, meters, charts, split screens, zooms.

## 6. Sound

- **Music:** continuous bed under VO. Mean loudness -12 to -16 dB for stock templates, against -21 to -26 dB for talking-head and quiet text-card videos.
- **Silence:** silencedetect (-35 dB, >0.4 s) finds no gaps in five of seven. B8u97x5Ggs4 has a 4.1 s gap at 54.4-58.5 s, before its end card. NjaSrUN0ozw has a 0.5 s gap at the start only.
- **SFX on text, risers:** not verifiable from our analysis. Treat as unverified.

## 7. Twelve rules for our reels (ranked by impact on retention)

1. Put the claim on screen in the first 2 s: a number or "you" line (fO9Q, NjaS).
2. Keep the audio bed continuous. Do not leave silence longer than 0.5 s.
3. Cut every 0.3-1 s during B-roll, or the stock template rhythm shows (fO9Q, NFyB).
4. Use one persistent header label for the topic (fO9Q, lB2L).
5. Number the points and give each its own card (tXao, fO9Q).
6. Keep captions to 3-6 words at mid-screen, one highlight word per card.
7. Use a glow flash as the transition between beats, every 2-4 s (NFyB).
8. Open with an animated element in frame 1 (E47). For us: silhouette glow animation.
9. End with a subscribe/bell card of 3-5 s, not a loop (B8u97x5Ggs4, fO9Q).
10. Keep the reel faceless: VO plus visuals. Talking heads are the non-faceless exclusions here.
11. Put a 1-second visual punch on each reveal word (NFyB flash, tXao numeral).
12. Source all visuals from a clean library. The stock templates here are watermarked (KineMaster, Knowledgefacts24, PSYCHOMINDLOGY), a licensing risk.
