# Sourcing visual assets for dark-psychology Reels

Research date: 2026-10-12. "Verified" = read on the cited page. "Unverified" = search snippet, memory, or a page that could not be fetched.

## 1. Pinterest: why not to repost

- **Terms (verified):** ToS s.2(a) bars scraping and automated access "without our express prior permission". s.3(a): users "retain all rights" in what they post and may not post infringing content. s.3(b) licenses Pinterest and its users to distribute User Content, not you. Source: https://policy.pinterest.com/en/terms-of-service
- **Copyright:** Pins are user uploads, often movie stills, artwork or stock. Pinterest grants no free-to-use license, so assume they are not free.
- **API v5 limits (verified):** Trial: 1,000 requests/day for all calls. Standard: 100 requests/second per user per app. Read endpoints (boards, pins) are 1,000/day on Trial and 1,000/min per user on Standard. Source: https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/reference/rate-limits.md. Standard access needs app review with a demo video (search snippet, unverified).
- **Risk (unverified, general knowledge):** Reposting Pinterest images or movie clips can draw a rightsholder DMCA report. Instagram may remove the Reel, flag the account, or make the Reel ineligible for monetization. No primary source fetched.

**Verdict:** Use Pinterest only as a mood-board source of search terms, not as a download source.

## 3. Fit for dark / moody / psychology look

- **Pexels, Pixabay:** Mood footage. Search: "dark room", "silhouette shadow", "fog figure", "night rain window", "smoke darkness", "dim hallway", "red light dark", "mirror reflection", "puppet strings", "surveillance camera".
- **Coverr:** Slow cinematic loops. Search: "dark loop", "moody cinematic", "neon noir", "night city rain".
- **Wikimedia Commons:** Historic psychology material. Search: "Milgram", "Stanford prison experiment", "phrenology", "Pavlov", "psychology illustration". Check each license.
- **Met, Rijksmuseum:** Chiaroscuro painting for surreal collage plates. Search: "Caravaggio", "Rembrandt night", "vanitas", "memento mori", "Goya black paintings".
- **Internet Archive:** Old educational psychology films. Search: "Prelinger", "psychology film 1950s". Verify rights first.

## 4. Credit

- **None required:** Pexels, Pixabay, Coverr, Unsplash, CC0 items (credit appreciated).
- **Required:** Wikimedia CC BY / BY-SA; Internet Archive items marked "with attribution".
- **Check per item:** Mixkit, Videvo, Smithsonian, Rijksmuseum, Internet Archive.

## Summary

Use Pexels and Pixabay (free keys) for mood footage, Wikimedia and CC0 museum sets for illustration and surreal plates. Avoid Pinterest downloads and uncleared film clips. Keep a per-asset log of URL, license and date.
