# The Dark Needle — visual grammar (watched by the main agent, 2026-10-13)

Sources: EhTTwjSKgL4 (How Women Decide Which Men They Want), NSqRpLnmLO8 (The Brutal Reason She Does Not Respect You).
Frames: 1 fps 0–12 s, every 3 s to 2:30. Labels below are frame ids from those sheets (a = second, b = 12 s + 3 s·n).

## Stage
- Background is **never flat black**: very dark navy/charcoal with a faint texture — a fine grid (EhT all) or soft wavy
  vertical lines (NSq all), plus a gentle light falloff.
- No floor, no reflections. Figures float in the dark space.

## Figures
- Solid white silhouettes with a soft white glow (EhT a01–a03). Big, often **cropped by the frame edge** and off-centre,
  with depth from size: a large foreground pair + a small figure behind (EhT a05–a09).
- **Focus**: the subject gets a coloured rim glow (NSq b08–b16 yellow/orange), others dim or go soft/blurred (NSq b03 blurred figure behind the narrator).
- Secondary characters are **pictograms** (AIGA-style round-head people, armchair, desk) in white or one colour (EhT b29–b33, NSq b13 green, b17 orange).
- Crowds as set pieces: a line of silhouettes, one highlighted in colour (EhT b40–b45 yellow), a circle of seated figures (NSq b34).
- A narrator mascot (suit, round head with a needle) points at things with a stick (EhT a12, NSq b06).

## Text
- Condensed bold uppercase, white, **placed in the scene** next to the subject, not in a fixed caption bar (EhT a06–a09, NSq a08).
- Builds word by word / item by item **as it is said** (EhT a06 JOB → a07 MONEY → a08 FACE → HOW YOU TREAT PEOPLE).
- One accent per phrase in colour (NSq b02 "EXCEPT YOU" red; EhT b36 "MAN BEHAVING" yellow); strike-through in colour (EhT b17).
- Big text can fly in with motion blur (NSq b03–b04); text alone on the dark for a beat ("PRACTICE" EhT b39; "SHE IS CAREFUL WITH EVERYONE EXCEPT YOU" NSq b02).

## Lines & graphics
- **Thin** lines only: dashed curved arrows with small heads carry messages/attention (NSq a03 red, a08 teal); thin solid
  curved arrows link ideas (EhT b22–b25); "=" and "→" make visual equations (EhT b04–b05).
- Thin outlined boxes (white, yellow, red) frame groups/panels; elbow connector lines build a mind-map (NSq b25–b31).
- Magnifying-glass icon for "scanning/judging" (EhT b18–b25); progress bar 0% red → yellow (NSq b43); puppet strings (NSq b11).

## UI & props
- Realistic phone lock screen with message notifications carries dialogue (NSq a01–a07: "My Love — Hey.. I can't make it tonight" vs "BOSS — be ready in 30 minutes").
- Speech text sits as small white caps beside the speaker (NSq a08 "ABSOLUTELY. I'LL BE READY.").

## Camera & motion
- Builds in place; the camera pans/zooms across a bigger layout to reveal the whole diagram (EhT b06 → b15).
- Background elements defocus to push attention to text (NSq a03 phone blurs when the text arrives).

## Ours = his grammar + one brand colour
Every coloured accent (red, yellow, teal, green, orange) becomes **purple #B26BFF**. Everything else stays white on the dark textured stage.
