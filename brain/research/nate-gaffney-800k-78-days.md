# Nate Gaffney: 10k → 800k Instagram followers in 78 days (YouTube dTxYPhPhtw8, watched 10-27)

1. **Trial reels + "five method":** one idea, 5 executions (same spot, quick to make), each with a different scripted text hook
   and a differently worded body. Same body + new hook gets flagged as a duplicate and not pushed. Trial reels go to
   non-followers only, so the result is objective data. Flops = data, not failure. He posted 10–15 trial reels a day.
2. **Make people feel seen:** the hook names a specific person, experience or emotion ("intellectual omnivore") so the viewer
   asks "is that me / my partner?". Say who it's for and why to watch. Talk like a FaceTime call with a friend
   ("Have you ever noticed…"), not a lecture. That drives sends, saves and comments.
3. **Series = the reason to follow:** same idea as standalone: 3.5M views, 13k followers. Re-made as "episode 1" of a named
   series: 2.4M views, 57k followers (4x followers on fewer views).
4. **Infrastructure before the blow-up:** landing page, a free product to collect emails, paid products.
