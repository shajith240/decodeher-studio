# Editing reference — "premium editing" reel (Pinterest, owner-shared 10-13)

Source: https://pin.it/4QcbPGXum (80 s, his 7-step editing cheat code). The owner likes his caption style: **not metallic, still premium**.

## His 7 steps → what we take
1. **Program** — any editor. We use HyperFrames.
2. **Trim silences** (he names FireCut / Gling). We already control every gap in reel.json; the voice is never padded.
3. **Storytelling first** — the edit serves the story. This matches [[relatable-storytelling]].
4. **Music** — low and under the voice. Already in [[audio]].
5. **B-roll: "if I say donuts, I should see donuts."** We show what is said through cast poses, WhatsApp bubbles, and emoji (pending owner approval).
6. **Text** — fonts from fonts.google.com / dafont.com; animated text packs from motionarray.com, creativemarket.com, elements.envato.com.
   - Those marketplaces are paid or licence-bound: check the licence before using anything. Google Fonts is free (OFL).
7. **Colour grading** — one consistent look. Ours is the black studio with purple rim light.

## His caption look (adopted as `DN.TIERS`)
- Two tiers per phrase: a smaller white top line, then the KEY words below, bigger, in the accent colour (ours is purple #B26BFF).
- Heavy **bold italic uppercase**, centred. We use Montserrat 900 italic, the closest match in the font comparison.
- Flat colour with a soft drop shadow and a gentle glow on the key line. No chrome, no metallic gradient.
- Each word EASES in on its spoken time (frame study at 30 fps): it slides in ~30 px from the right, fades from grey to white over ~6 frames, and flickers once in brightness. It is not a wipe and has no bounce. Each phrase cuts to the next.
- No punctuation on screen.
- Flash transitions (white or light-leak) at section changes and numbered badges: not adopted yet.
