# Relatable storytelling for dating reels

Research only. Sources are marked. Anything without a source is practitioner convention and is flagged **[unverified]**.

## Evidence base
- **Ranking signals:** Mosseri (Jan 2025): "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." Sends matter slightly more for non-followers. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/
- **Median engagement:** Socialinsider, 15M posts, Oct 2025 to Mar 2026: Reels median 35 saves, 5 shares. Vendor study, not peer reviewed. https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-engagement-report
- **Narrative transportation:** Green and Brock (2000, JPSP): the more absorbed a reader is in a story, the more they adopt its beliefs. A later meta-analysis (Van Laer et al., 2012) finds transportation rises with identifiable characters and a believable plot. Read from search summaries, not full papers.
- **Follow/tag prompts:** Fanpage Karma (540 Reels): prompts to follow, tag or buy cut reach 60-65%. Methodology not verified. https://blog.fanpagekarma.com/?p=22627
- **Identity drives shares:** BuzzFeed's Dao Nguyen names identity, emotion and conversation as main drivers of shareability (cited secondhand). https://digimedia.be/News/NL/17843/the-art-of-shareability.html

Hook percentages, "re-hook every 5-8 s" and the 3-second drop-off claims are **[unverified]**. No study tested them.

## 1. Hooks (first 1-3 s)
| Technique | Example A | Example B |
|---|---|---|
| POV "you" scene | "POV: you send 'haha' and she replies 'lol'." | "POV: it's 2am and her 'k' is still the last thing on your screen." |
| Specific mundane detail | "Her reply took four hours. You timed it." | "Two bars of signal, one 'you good?', one thumbs-up from a girl you met once." |
| Mid-action start | "...and you're already typing the third follow-up." | "...so you hit send before you can rewrite it again." |
| Pattern interrupt | "Stop texting her first. Here's what happens." | "Your best message is the one you don't send." |
| "Nobody talks about this" | "Nobody warns you: the girl who flirts hardest won't always date you." | "Nobody tells you the girl who likes you will talk about the guy she wants." |
| Calling out the exact situation | "She's always busy, but always answers at midnight. Watch this." | "You've been the 'nice guy' in her group chat for three months." |

Examples are mine, written for this brief.

## 2. Beat structure (35 s)
Beat timings are **[unverified]** convention.
1. **0-3 s Hook:** silhouette mid-action plus one line.
2. **3-8 s Scene:** second-person, two concrete details.
3. **8-15 s Tension:** name the inner excuse ("you tell yourself it's fine"), one line of dialogue.
4. **15-21 s Turn:** the moment it goes wrong. Show it, do not explain it.
5. **21-28 s Mechanism:** one plain claim about what is happening.
6. **28-33 s Move:** one line or action he can copy.
7. **33-35 s Payoff/loop:** callback to the hook, last words lead back into the hook's first word.

Re-hook (new visual or new line) every 5-8 s **[unverified]**.

## 3. Save vs share vs follow
- **Share ("this is so him"):** a line the viewer would send to a friend. Sends are a top-three ranking signal (Mosseri), so this is the main reach lever.
- **Save ("I need this later"):** an exact script or checklist. Reels saves are high in the Socialinsider data, so a usable line earns the save.
- **Follow ("what's next"):** a series promise, not a request. The Fanpage Karma result argues against "follow me" prompts.

Natural CTA lines (mine, untested):
- "Send this to the friend who keeps saying 'she's just busy.'"
- "Save this for the next time she goes quiet."
- "Part 2 is what to say when she calls you out. Next week."

## 4. Retention
- Open a loop in the first 3 s, close it in the final fifth **[unverified, practitioner]**. https://www.socialync.io/blog/open-loops-in-content-2026 (blog)
- Vary the loop type. Repeating "wait for it" trains viewers to skip it (same source).
- Specificity: times, apps, objects. Dialogue in quotes breaks the narrator's voice.
- The last line answers the loop and reopens it.

## 5. How The Dark Needle makes it relatable
I read three transcripts (2026-10-11, 2026-08-29, 2026-07-01). His videos run 10-13 minutes, so copy the method, not the length.
- **Starts in a scene with a stranger or a cancellation.** 07-01: "At the last minute, she cancels on you without even saying sorry." The viewer is already in it.
- **Second person, then self-deprecation.** 08-29: "This is you, a stupid average guy walking down the street." He puts himself in the viewer's shoes, then makes them the "average guy."
- **Makes the viewer imagine a real person.** 07-01: "Think about your boss right now." The viewer does the work, which makes it feel personal.
- **Names the viewer's inner excuse.** 10-11 opens on "a girl you've probably met... who's going to waste a lot of your time." The viewer recognizes an ex or a current girl.
- **Own story as proof.** 10-11 at 10:02: "I lost a year of my life because of her. So, please don't be like me."
- **Opens a loop early.** 07-01: "There is one reason she does not respect you... it is going to sound strange when I say it. That is why I'm not going to say it yet."

Caution: several of his lines are absolute claims ("Whoever chases loses"). For our page, keep the mechanism honest and the claim narrow.

## Rules for our scripts
1. Open on a specific second-person moment within 3 s. No intro, no name.
2. One situation per reel. One named feeling per beat.
3. Put a concrete detail (time, app, object) in the first 5 s.
4. Show the turn. Narrate only its meaning.
5. Put a line of dialogue in every 8 s beat.
6. Re-hook (new visual or line) every 5-8 s.
7. Open a loop in the first 3 s. Close it in the final fifth.
8. Explain one mechanism in plain words. No "studies show."
9. Give one usable line or move, so the viewer has something to save.
10. The viewer's blind spot is the subject, not the girl. No lecturing from above.
11. The last line calls back to the hook and flows into its first word.
12. One CTA sentence, no "follow me" and no tag prompts, in the video or the caption.

## Template
```
[HOOK 0-3s]     "POV: {specific moment, with time/place/app}."
[SCENE 3-8s]    "{Second-person detail}. {Second detail}."
[TENSION 8-15s] "You tell yourself {excuse}. '{one line of dialogue}.'"
[TURN 15-21s]   "Then {the concrete thing that goes wrong}."
[MECHANISM 21-28s] "What's actually happening: {one plain claim}."
[MOVE 28-33s]   "Instead, {one line or one action}."
[PAYOFF 33-35s] "{Callback to hook}. {Last line that reopens into the hook's first word}."
[CTA]           "Send this to {the friend who...}" or "Save this for {next trigger}."
```

Unverified: hook benchmarks, beat timings, re-hook interval, the 20% loop-close rule, and any claim that saves or shares beat watch time for a dating niche.
