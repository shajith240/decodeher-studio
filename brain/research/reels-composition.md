# Reel composition on a 1080×1920 canvas

Confidence tags: [S] = sourced from a third-party guide; [D] = derived by us from sourced figures; [U] = unverified.
No Meta or Instagram help page on organic Reels safe zones was found. The Meta figures found are for ads only.

## 1. Safe zones and crops

- **Top 250–270 px** [S]: handle and audio bar. Argil gives 250 px ([argil.ai](https://argil.ai/blog/instagram-reel-size-e350f)); Hopper HQ gives 14% ≈ 270 px ([hopperhq.com](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/)). Safest: 270 px.
- **Bottom 250–670 px** [S]: caption, audio, action row. Argil says 250 px; ScreenSnap says 350 px ([screensnap.pro](https://www.screensnap.pro/blog/instagram-reels-size-guide)); Hopper and the Meta-ads figure say 35% ≈ 670 px. Safest: 670 px for text.
- **Right column x 850–1080** [S/U]: the action rail. Hopper says the rightmost 21% (≈230 px) is covered. Its vertical extent is unverified; assume y 1100–1700.
- **Sides** [S]: 65 px (6%) per Hopper; Argil says keep 100 px from the left edge.
- **Center safe box**: Argil 1080×1420; ContentStudio 880×1420 ([contentstudio.io](https://contentstudio.io/blog/instagram-reel-size)); Hopper ~950×980. Our text box: x 100–980, y 270–1250 [D].
- **Profile grid**: most sources say 3:4 (1080×1440), which hides 240 px top and bottom, so visible y 240–1680 ([oktopost.com](https://www.oktopost.com/blog/instagram-grid-size-guide/)). Argil says 1:1 from the center, y 420–1500. Sources disagree; the band y 420–1500 satisfies both [D].
- **Feed preview**: one source says 4:5 (y ≈ 285–1635). Unverified.

## 2. Eyes, text, and size

- **Eyes**: top third line, y ≈ 640 [S, generic video guidance: Hamilton University, [PDF](https://academics.hamilton.edu/mediascholarship/sitePages/files/NITLE%20MIV/MHhome_video.pdf)]. The 9:16 figure is [U].
- **Thirds grid**: x 360 / 720, y 640 / 1280 [D].
- **Text placement**: upper-middle, y 380–700, below the header and above the feet. Keep it out of the bottom 670 px.
- **Caption size**: 60–75 px is the vendor consensus; 48 px is the floor for dialogue ([blitzcutai.com](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-size-youtube-shorts-2026)). Treat as [S, vendor].
- **Phone size check** [D]: 60 px on a 1080 px frame ≈ 22 pt on a 390 pt-wide phone.
- **Line length**: 32–37 characters per line for vertical subtitles [S].

## 3. Subject size and framing

- No sourced figures for full-body subject height. Everything below is [U] or [D]:
- **One standing figure**: 800 px tall (700–900 px range), head top y ≈ 420, feet y ≈ 1220–1250.
- **Two people talking**: 700–800 px each, side by side, heads on the same line y ≈ 420–500, feet y ≈ 1250. Keep the gap between figures ≥ 150 px.
- **Headroom**: ≥ 420 px above the head, so the head clears the grid crop.
- **Lead room**: ≥ 300 px in the direction the figure faces. A figure facing right sits centered at x ≈ 360.
- **Feet**: keep inside the frame at y ≤ 1250. Cropping at the knee or ankle reads as an accident, not a choice [U].

## 4. Amateur mistakes

- Text inside the top 270 px or the bottom 670 px.
- Subject dead-center with no lead room.
- Head at the top edge, or chin cut at a joint.
- Captions under 48 px, or more than 37 characters per line.
- Anything important in the right column.
- Feet at the bottom edge with no reason.
- Cover never checked in the 3:4 grid preview.
- More than one focal point per frame.

## Rules for our reels

1. All text lives in x 100–980, y 270–1250.
2. Nothing essential sits above y 270, or in x ≥ 850 for y 1100–1700.
3. y ≥ 1250 is background and glow only; no text, no feet.
4. Standing full-body figure: 700–900 px tall (target 800), feet at y ≤ 1250. Our earlier example of feet at y ≈ 1500 conflicts with the bottom-35% figure, so it is replaced.
5. Head tops and titles fall within y 420–1500 (the grid-safe band).
6. Headroom: head top at y ≥ 420.
7. Lead room: subject center at x 360 facing right or x 720 facing left, with ≥ 300 px of space ahead.
8. Eyes at y 600–680.
9. Captions 60–70 px, never under 48 px, max 37 characters per line.
10. Before publishing, export the cover and check the 3:4 band (y 240–1680) and the 1:1 band (y 420–1500).
