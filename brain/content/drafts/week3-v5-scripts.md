Then turn it into a plan. [casual] You should meet him. Are you free Sunday?
When a girl remembers your small things, she's paying attention to you.

## 10 · Signs she likes you #5 · "Count the signs"
She touches your arm while you talk. [pause] And you freeze.
Most guys read way too much into it, or way too little, and the moment passes.
[long pause] Here's why. [lower voice] One touch can be friendliness, because some people touch everyone, so signs only count when they come together.
So count. Does she stand close, hold eye contact, and find reasons to touch you again?
If it's two or three, ask her in person before you leave. [casual] I like talking to you. Can I take you out sometime?
Asking takes ten seconds. Guessing takes weeks.
Save this for the next time a girl touches your arm.
[confident] One sign is a maybe. Three together are worth a question.
