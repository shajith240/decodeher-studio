## 02 · Dark psychology #35 · "Six words"
If a girl ever says these six words, she likes you. [pause] Listen.
Studies on flirting found that a woman's voice changes around someone she likes. Her pitch goes up, and her tone gets softer.
So listen to how she says these six words around you. Hi. Yeah. Really. Stop. No. Bye.
With a friend, or someone she's just comfortable with, they come out flat and quick.
But if she likes you, those same words turn sing-song and playful, and they stretch out into a teasing version.

## 03 · Dark psychology #36 · "Biggest turn-off"
I'm going to say this loud and clear. [pause] Listen. If she's hot and cold, flaky, and sends one-word texts, that has to be a turn-off.
I can't stress this enough. If everyone made this one shift inside, it would save love lives all over the world.
Someone being distant and unsure about you shouldn't make you chase harder. It should make you think, this is boring. Because it is.
You want someone who gets excited about weekend plans with you, who smiles when your name pops up on her phone.
If that isn't your standard, your standards are too low.
A relationship where you're always trying to keep her attention is a recipe for unhappiness.
So see it for what it is. A huge turn-off.
