## 01 · Dark psychology #22 · "Emojis"
If you want to know how to tell if a girl likes you over text, stop counting her emojis. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, because an emoji costs her nothing.
Look at her effort instead. Say you write, "I finally tried that ramen place." If she answers "Nice", that's a closed door. If she answers, "Wait, was it good? I've wanted to go," she gave you a question back.
Psychology calls it reciprocity. People who like you tend to give back what you give.
[confident] So check three things. Does she ask you questions. Does she bring up something of her own. Does she hint at a next step, like a place or a day.
One short reply means little. Three signs together mean a lot.

## 02 · Dark psychology #23 · "Hard to get"
Why do girls play hard to get when they like you? Most guys chase harder, and that's the mistake. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, because often she isn't playing at all.
Psychology calls it ambivalence. She's drawn to you, but she isn't sure yet that you're safe to get close to.
When you chase harder, you add pressure, so she pulls back. That looks like a game, but it's her protecting herself.
[confident] Here's what to do.
Number one. Stay warm and relaxed, and keep your own life going.
Number two. Make one clear, easy plan, like "Coffee Thursday at five?"
Number three. If she says no twice without offering another day, believe her and let it go.
A real yes doesn't need chasing.
[lower voice] Save this for the next time she feels hard to read.

## 03 · Dark psychology #24 · "Story views"
She watches your story every day, but doesn't text you. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, because it can mean three different things.
Psychology calls it passive attention. Viewing a story is the smallest thing she can do on the app, so it shows curiosity, not effort.
One, she's curious but shy. Two, she's keeping an eye on you without committing. Three, she's waiting for you to move first.
[confident] Here's how to find out.
Number one. Message her about something real, like her week.
Number two. Judge her answer. A reply with a question back is a good sign.
Number three. If nothing comes back after two tries, stop checking who views your story.
A view is attention. A reply is effort.
[lower voice] Send this to the guy who checks who watched his story.

## 04 · Dark psychology #25 · "Talking stage"
Do you want to know how to keep a girl interested in the talking stage? Ask yourself three questions before your next text. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, because most chats don't die from one bad text.
They fade because nothing new happens. People pay attention to what's fresh, and a chat that repeats itself gets boring.
[confident] Here are the three questions.
One. Did I ask about her, or did I only talk about me?
Two. Does this text give her something easy and fun to answer?
Three. Have I talked about meeting, or is this chat living only on the phone?
If the answer is no, fix that text first.
A clear plan keeps it alive better than a clever message.
[lower voice] Save this and run the three questions before your next text.

## 05 · Dark psychology #26 · "Space"
When she says she needs space but keeps texting you, don't answer the way most guys do. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, because the easy reply is the wrong one.
She writes, "I need some space right now." Two days later she sends, "Did you see that video?" And he replies, "Make up your mind, do you want space or not?"
[confident] Freeze.
That reply puts her on trial. She may want less pressure, not zero contact. Calling it out makes her defend herself.
Here's the rewrite. Say once, "Got it, take the room you need, text me when you want to." Then answer her warmly and keep it short.
If weeks pass, say it calmly. "I'm happy to give you space, but I need to know where we stand."
[lower voice] Send this to the friend who answers "I need space" with ten messages.

## 06 · Dark psychology #27 · "Dry texting"
Her texts used to be full sentences, and now she's suddenly dry texting you, one word at a time. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, because a change like that deserves a question, not a guess.
Psychology calls it a baseline shift. You compare her to her own normal, and the change is the signal.
It can be a stressful week or less interest. One dry day can't tell you which.
[confident] Here's what to do.
Number one. Think about her week. Exams and work can dry up anyone's texting.
Number two. Send one easy, specific message, like "How did that presentation go?" Not "why are you being dry".
Number three. If it stays dry for about a week, stop starting chats and put your energy elsewhere.
Her effort tells you where you stand.
[lower voice] Save this for the next time her texts go dry.
