## 01 · Decode her text #1 · "Haha"
You send her something funny, and she just sends a laugh back. [pause] Nothing else.
My friend sent a meme, she laughed, and he sent another. [pause] Then she stopped replying.
[long pause] Here's why. [lower voice] A meme doesn't ask her anything, so she has nothing to say back.
So bring up something she told you. Like, [casual] wait, how did your interview go?
Comment her last text, and I'll tell you what it means.
[confident] Give her something to answer, and she will.
