## 01 · Dark psychology #8 · "Haha"
She replies "haha" and nothing else. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, because it doesn't mean what you think.
Psychology calls it low-effort closure. A "haha" is a polite way to end a message when there's nothing left to answer.
It's not boredom. You just gave her nothing to reply to, because a joke isn't a question.
[confident] Here's what to do.
Number one. Don't send another joke. That only kills the chat faster.
Number two. Ask one real question about something she already told you.
Number three. If she answers in more than a few words, she's still in. If it's "haha" again, let it rest.
Chats don't die from one word. They die when nobody gives her a reason to talk.
[lower voice] Send this to the friend who gets "haha" and panics.

## 02 · Dark psychology #9 · "Three hours"
She takes three hours to reply, then sends a meme. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, because that's not random.
Psychology calls it low-pressure contact. A meme keeps the connection alive without asking her to think or commit.
She's busy or unsure, but she still wants you in her day.
[confident] Here's how you handle it.
Number one. Don't match her speed on purpose. Reply when you naturally would.
Number two. React to the meme, then add one real line of your own.
Number three. If she picks it up, move the chat toward a plan.
The guy who stays relaxed about her pace is the guy who's easy to talk to.
[lower voice] Save this for the next time she goes quiet.

## 03 · Dark psychology #10 · "Read it twice"
She read your text twice, then replied with just one short word. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, that pause means something.
Psychology calls it impression management. People choose their words carefully for the people they care what they think of.
If she didn't care, the first reply would have been instant and careless.
[confident] Here's how to read it.
Number one. Don't take a short reply as a rejection. Look at how carefully she wrote it.
Number two. Give her a clear, easy thing to respond to.
Number three. Stay warm and don't over-explain. Pressure makes careful people pull back.
Someone who thinks before she answers is usually someone who's still deciding.
[lower voice] Send this to the friend who reads her texts ten times before replying.

## 04 · Dark psychology #11 · "Double text then silence"
She double texts you once, and then goes quiet. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, don't chase her with a third message.
Psychology calls it reciprocity checking. She reached out twice, so now she's watching whether you meet her halfway.
Silence here isn't a no. It's her waiting to see what you do with the effort.
[confident] Here's how to answer it.
Number one. Reply to her, and acknowledge that she reached out.
Number two. Keep it light and specific. A real answer, not a paragraph.
Number three. Then stop. Don't send a follow-up until she answers.
When you respect her effort without smothering it, she feels safe to keep going.
[lower voice] Save this for the next time you want to send that third text.

## 05 · Dark psychology #12 · "We'll see"
When she says "we'll see", read what she does next. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, the words aren't the answer.
Psychology calls it behavioural commitment. What people do next tells you more than what they say, because actions cost something and words don't.
"We'll see" can mean maybe, or it can mean no without saying it.
[confident] Here's how to find out.
Number one. Don't push for a yes. Say, no problem, and move on.
Number two. Wait. See if she brings the plan back herself.
Number three. If she does, it's real. If she doesn't, you saved yourself a week of guessing.
Interest shows up in effort. If it never shows up, you have your answer.
[lower voice] Send this to the friend she just told "we'll see".

## 06 · Dark psychology #13 · "She never starts"
She never starts the conversation. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, because most guys get this wrong.
Psychology calls it the pursuit gap. When one person always makes the first move, the other gets comfortable replying and stops reaching.
It doesn't always mean she doesn't like you. It does mean she's not putting effort in yet.
[confident] Here's how to test it.
Number one. Skip one conversation. Don't start it, and see what happens.
Number two. If she reaches out, reward it with a warm, quick reply.
Number three. If days pass with nothing, stop carrying the whole chat alone.
You're not punishing her. You're just finding out if the interest goes both ways.
[lower voice] Save this, and check who started your last chat.
