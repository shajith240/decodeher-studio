## 01 · Dark psychology #58 · "Run from these three lines"
Run if someone says one of these three things early in dating. [pause] Listen.
Number one is when she says, "I'm not looking for anything serious, but I like spending time with you." What she really means is that she wants you on her terms, without any commitment.
Number two is when she tells you, "You're different from anyone I've ever met, I think you might be the one." That's love bombing, which means far too much affection, far too early, so if you've only known her a week, be careful.
Number three is when she says, "I've been hurt a lot, so I struggle to trust people." Too often she's using her past as an excuse to dodge responsibility, because everyone has been hurt.
So these aren't just red flags, they're dealbreakers.
[lower voice] Send this to the friend who ignores the early signs.

## 02 · Dark psychology #59 · "Four things she finds unattractive"
There are four things women find unattractive, and most men do at least one. [pause] Listen.
Number one is putting yourself down. If you don't see your own value, why would she, so practise kinder self-talk before a date.
Number two is neglecting your health. You don't need to live in the gym, but staying active and eating well shows her that you value yourself.
Number three is poor hygiene or grooming. So keep yourself clean, because women are drawn to men who take pride in how they look.
Number four is a lack of ambition. Women are drawn to men with drive and goals, so work on something that is yours.
[lower voice] Save this as a checklist before your next date.
