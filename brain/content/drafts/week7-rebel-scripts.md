## 01 · Dark psychology #2 · "Seen then story"
She leaves you on seen, then likes your story an hour later. [pause] [lower voice] Listen to this.
Psychology calls it breadcrumbing. Small crumbs of attention, just enough to keep you hoping, never enough to go anywhere.
It works because your brain treats every crumb like a sign, and you keep waiting for the next one.
[confident] Here's how you handle it.
Number one. Don't reply to the like. A like is not a message, so it doesn't get an answer.
Number two. Watch what she does, not what she taps. Plans and real conversations count. Hearts don't.
Number three. Make one clear move. Ask her out once, with a day and a time.
If she says yes, great. If she dodges, you have your answer, and you stop collecting crumbs.
[lower voice] Send this to the friend who keeps decoding her likes.

## 02 · Dark psychology #3 · "Sometime"
She says, we should hang out sometime. [pause] [lower voice] Listen carefully, because most guys hear a yes.
Psychology calls this face-saving. People soften a no so nobody feels rejected, and "sometime" is the softest no there is.
A real yes always comes with a detail. A day, a place, a time she's free.
[confident] So here's what you do.
Number one. Turn sometime into something real. Say, cool, how about Thursday evening?
Number two. Listen to her answer, not her tone. "Thursday works" is interest. "I'll let you know" usually isn't.
Number three. If she's vague twice, stop asking. Leave the door open and get on with your week.
The guy who can hear a soft no without chasing it is the guy she remembers.
[lower voice] Follow, because next I'll show you the three words that mean she's actually in.

## 03 · Dark psychology #4 · "Ask her a favour"
Want her to like you more? [pause] Don't do her a favour. [lower voice] Let her do one for you. Listen.
Psychology calls this the Benjamin Franklin effect. When someone does something small for you, their brain decides they must like you.
Because nobody wants to believe they helped a person they don't care about.
[confident] Here's how to use it the right way.
Number one. Ask for her opinion. Which jacket, which song, which place for dinner.
Number two. Keep it small. A favour that takes ten seconds, never one that costs her time or money.
Number three. Thank her once, and actually use what she told you.
Now she's invested in you a little, and people value what they've invested in.
[lower voice] Save this, and try it on your next conversation.

## 04 · Dark psychology #5 · "She stopped asking"
She still replies. [pause] But she stopped asking you anything. [lower voice] That's the real warning sign. Listen.
Psychology calls it reciprocal disclosure. When someone is interested, they ask questions back, because they want to know you.
When the questions stop, the conversation is running on your energy alone.
[confident] Here's what to do.
Number one. Count her questions, not her replies. Fast answers mean nothing if nothing comes back.
Number two. Stop carrying the chat. Send one good message and let the silence do the work.
Number three. If she comes back curious, great. If she doesn't, you saved yourself weeks.
Interest always asks. Politeness only answers.
[lower voice] Follow, because next I'll show you the one question that brings her curiosity back.

## 05 · Dark psychology #6 · "Why chasing fails"
The more you chase her, the faster she pulls away. [pause] [lower voice] It's not bad luck. Listen.
Psychology calls this the scarcity principle. People want what's hard to get, and lose interest in what's always available.
When you reply in seconds and cancel your plans for her, you're telling her brain you're easy to get.
[confident] Here's how you flip it.
Number one. Have a life she has to fit into. Gym, friends, work, plans you don't drop.
Number two. Answer when you're free, not the moment her name pops up.
Number three. Give attention fully when you're together, then go back to your day.
You're not playing hard to get. You're just a man with something going on.
[lower voice] Send this to the friend who replies in two seconds.

## 06 · Dark psychology #7 · "The small test"
She teases you, just a little, to see what you'll do. [pause] [lower voice] Listen, because this is a test.
Psychology calls it a congruence test. She's checking if your confidence is real or just an act.
Most guys fail it two ways. They get offended, or they start explaining themselves.
[confident] Here's how you pass.
Number one. Don't defend. Smile, and agree in a playful way.
Number two. Tease back lightly, then move the conversation on.
Number three. Never ask, are you mad at me? Calm is the answer she's looking for.
A man who can laugh at himself is a man who doesn't need her approval, and that's attractive.
[lower voice] Comment the last thing she teased you about, and I'll tell you what to say.
