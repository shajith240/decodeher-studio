## 03 · Dark psychology #52 · "When she looks at you"
When a woman looks at you, don't do this. [pause] Listen. Don't look away fast because you're scared of eye contact.
Instead, when she looks your way, hold her eye contact and give her a friendly smile.
A lot of guys avoid eye contact because they think it's creepy, but holding eye contact and staring are two very different things.
You've done nothing wrong by holding her eyes and smiling, because it's a friendly opener and a great way to read her interest.
If she smiles back or holds your gaze, that's a strong sign she wants you to come over. But if she looks away fast or doesn't smile, that's fine, so don't approach her.
So next time a woman looks your way, don't panic, because it's a good thing. Smile, and hold her eyes.
