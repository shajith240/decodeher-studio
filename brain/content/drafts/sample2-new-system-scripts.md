## 01 · Dark psychology #15 · "She cancelled"
She cancels your date an hour before, and just says "something came up." [pause]
Your mind jumps straight to the worst answer.
But one cancel can mean two very different things.
Maybe life really got in the way. Or maybe she's losing interest.
Here's how you tell the difference. Watch what she does next.
If she's interested, she offers you a new day, without you asking.
If she's not, she says sorry, and leaves the whole plan in your hands.
So reply once, and keep it easy. "No problem. Let me know what day works for you."
If she picks a day, great. If she doesn't, you have your answer, and you never had to beg for it.
[lower voice] Save this for the next time a plan falls through.
