TOPIC: social-proof

CORE INSIGHT: The vault claims women sort men by the strongest signals he broadcasts, and that other people's interest in a man works as a filter that is hard to fake.

RELATABLE SITUATION: He is the always-available, agreeable guy. She talks to him warmly and treats him as a friend. He watches other men get chosen and assumes he lost on looks or money.

WHY IT HAPPENS: Per the vault, attraction is an automatic scan that returns a result before conscious thought. Loud provider signals (safe, eager, helpful) get him filed as "friend," and visible interest from others is an expensive-to-fake shortcut.

THE MOVE: Stop comparing yourself to the man she picked. Name one signal you send too loudly (eagerness, agreeing with everything) and cut it this week.

LIMITS: "Women scan and file men automatically" is the creator's model, not a study. Don't promise crowd interest makes any woman chase him. Skip the "they'll fight for you" framing and its withholding tactics.

TOPIC: commitment

CORE INSIGHT: The vault claims that after a woman commits, men tend to give up what drew her in (open schedule, standards, boundaries), and that she then tests whether he is still solid.

RELATABLE SITUATION: He cancels his friends and plans to be with her, texts all day, and when she pushes on something small he folds and apologises. She starts testing him, or pulls back.

WHY IT HAPPENS: Per 2026-03-14, unlimited availability makes his time worthless, and once he stops being "the prize" she feels he is beneath her. Folding under pressure reads as losing solidity. 2025-07-23 adds that men want what they have to pursue.

THE MOVE: Keep one plan of your own each week. When she asks for a busy day, say "I have plans, how about Thursday?" with no apology.

LIMITS: The vault's "90% of relationships fail for this reason" is an unsourced figure; don't repeat it. "Withdraw rewards when effort drops" is the creator's advice, not a rule of healthy love. Don't imply every test is manipulation.

SOURCE NOTES: 2026-03-14 I'm Begging You to Stop Making These Mistakes With Women; 2025-07-23 If she's sending you mixed signals, do this NOW; Topics/commitment.md

TOPIC: judgment-fear

CORE INSIGHT: The vault claims a woman's dating and sexual rules are defences against being judged (by people around her, by him, and by herself), and that they loosen when she feels no judgment.

RELATABLE SITUATION: At a bar she is warm and flirty with him, but when he leans in she says "not tonight," or mentions a boyfriend. He takes it as rejection or starts over-explaining himself.

WHY IT HAPPENS: Per 2026-10-04, her feelings come first and her reasons come after. Her rules protect her from being seen as easy. The stronger the attraction, the less she needs an excuse to break a rule.

THE MOVE: When she slows down, take it as her answer: "No problem, I like hanging out with you," with no joke about her being prude or easy. Keep the night relaxed.

LIMITS: "Hesitation means she cares what you think, which is a good sign" is the creator's reading, not a fact. Never coach getting around a no or making her feel pressured to break a rule. The boldness video's claims (embarrassment is "chemical," people are "NPCs") are unsupported.

SOURCE NOTES: 2026-10-04 Why Women Break Their Own Rules for Certain Men; 2025-06-04 Being the BOLD Guy is Insanely Simple; Topics/judgment-fear.md

TOPIC: focus

CORE INSIGHT: The vault claims constant phone and music stimulation scatters attention, and that deliberate low-stimulation time (one task, phone away, some boredom) rebuilds it.

RELATABLE SITUATION: He sits down to study or work, opens his phone "for one second," then music, and forty minutes are gone. By night nothing is finished and he feels behind.

WHY IT HAPPENS: 2024-06-14 says any phone glance breaks concentration, and 2024-06-09 says the mind wanders productively when bored, citing Newton and Einstein. 2024-06-26 calls music a dopamine habit. These are the creator's points, not measurements.

THE MOVE: Put the phone in another room. Do one 25-minute block on one task, then a 5-minute break with no screen.

LIMITS: The vault gives no data. Don't repeat "10 minutes lost" or "90-second urges" as facts, the Newton/Einstein story as evidence, or the binaural-beats advice as proven. "Music is addictive, quit it" is opinion. Avoid the word "dopamine" as a science claim.

SOURCE NOTES: 2024-06-09 OUTWORK everyone by being bored; 2024-06-14 It's actually pretty easy to get ahead of 99% of people; 2024-06-26 why i stopped listening to music; Topics/focus.md

TOPIC: female-psychology

CORE INSIGHT: The vault claims a woman's desire needs some uncertainty, the sense that he could leave, so a man who is always available and fully secured loses the pull even while she stays warm.

RELATABLE SITUATION: He texts "good morning" daily, replies in seconds, says yes to every plan, and is there whenever she is upset. She is close but stopped initiating, and when he asks to meet she says she is busy.

WHY IT HAPPENS: 2026-06-24 describes a "chooser" (logic) that picks a stable man and a "wanter" (desire) that needs loss to be possible. 2026-10-11 says attention given freely gets valued less. 2026-03-28 makes the same uncertainty claim.

THE MOVE: Stop texting first for a few days. Say once, "Can't this week, Saturday works?" and then let her answer.

LIMITS: "Chooser and wanter" and "uncertainty causes dopamine" are the creator's models, not verified findings. Don't call it universal, and don't say "she only wants a man she can lose." Don't tell him to write her off as "just wanting attention" (2026-10-11).

SOURCE NOTES: 2026-06-24 Why Women Suddenly Lose Interest in Good Men; 2026-10-11 Why She Wants Your Attention But Not You; 2026-03-28 The REAL Reason Women Lose Interest in You; Topics/female-psychology.md

TOPIC: communication

CORE INSIGHT: The vault claims texting and conversation go better when they are playful and not predictable, and that anxious over-texting and defensive replies both kill attraction.

RELATABLE SITUATION: He sends long messages and keeps asking "what are you up to?" and "what music do you like?", and he waits by the phone for her reply. She answers in one word and the chat dies.

WHY IT HAPPENS: 2026-07-18 says plain information-transfer texts feel like work, and a predictable reply pattern gets "solved." 2025-10-16 says fear leaks through and predictability kills tension. The vault suggests turning questions into playful statements.

THE MOVE: Swap one question for a playful observation ("You're the type who cries to sad songs and denies it") and don't reply to every message instantly.

LIMITS: The vault has no response-rate data, so "3-hour delays don't work" is its claim. The "broken vending machine" idea of random silence is manipulation framing; don't advise going quiet to create a hook. Texting must lead to meeting in person, not replace it.

SOURCE NOTES: 2026-07-18 This Texting Method Makes Women Addicted to You; 2025-10-16 No Blocking. No Begging.; 2026-03-28 The REAL Reason Women Lose Interest in You; Topics/communication.md

TOPIC: self-image

CORE INSIGHT: The vault claims people read a man's worth from how he carries himself, and that chasing approval, over-thanking, and saying yes to what he does not want all lower his standing.

RELATABLE SITUATION: He agrees to plans he does not want, thanks her five times for something small, and laughs along at jokes about himself. She runs late, cancels, and snaps at him, and he accepts it.

WHY IT HAPPENS: 2025-12-13 says people sense fake agreement and read excess kindness as low self-worth. 2026-07-01 says accepting bad behaviour tells her it needs no care. 2025-05-28 says seeking her approval leaks respect.

THE MOVE: Say no once this week to one thing you do not want, in one short sentence: "Can't make it, I'm doing X."

LIMITS: "People instantly punish fake behaviour" and "respect is self-control" are the creator's views. Don't say his self-image decides her attraction. Don't advise coldness or silent punishment. Don't blend "she disrespects you for what she gets to be around you" with "it is about who you are" (the vault says the former).
