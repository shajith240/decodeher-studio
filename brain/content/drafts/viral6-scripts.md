She says something came up and she can't make it. "No worries, let me know when you're free." Friend zone.
Instead: "No worries. What about tomorrow night?"

## 04 · Dark psychology #47 · "Stop chasing her"
Stop chasing her for a second, bro, and just listen. [pause]
If you really stop and look at the situation you're in, you're walking towards her while she's walking away from you.
Now, wouldn't it be better to flip it around?
You chase something else. Your studies, your business, your fitness, your goals. And she's the one chasing after you.
Sure, you stop for a moment to spend time with her. But mostly, it's like that, not the other way round.
Getting there is hard, because you have to let go of the old beliefs that made you chase in the first place.

## 05 · Dark psychology #48 · "Three rules of Thomas Shelby"
You can attract anyone in ten seconds without saying a word. [pause] Listen. Remember these three rules of Thomas Shelby, especially the last one.
Number one. Unshakeable posture. Keep your back straight and slow your walk down. People who've lost control of their lives hunch and hurry. Walking slow and steady shows dominance.
Number two. Eye contact. Most people look away within two seconds, because there's fear inside. Don't stare. Just look into their eyes, calmly, and say without words that you're in charge.
Number three. Respect on demand. Fix your posture, and the people who used to say "listen to me" start saying "yes, sir."
Change yourself, and the way the world treats you changes on its own.
[lower voice] Save this, and walk slower tomorrow.
