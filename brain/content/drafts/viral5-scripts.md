## 01 · Dark psychology #37 · "Likes you but hides it"
Signs a girl likes you but is trying not to show it. [pause] Listen.
Number one. More attention. She asks you questions, and she remembers small details from your conversations later on.
Number two. Her body language. She holds your gaze, or she looks away fast when you catch her looking.
Number three. She keeps ending up close to you, and she leans in when you talk.
Number four. Playful teasing. It's her way of getting your attention and starting a fun back-and-forth, and it shows she feels at ease with you.

## 04 · Dark psychology #40 · "Never do this around her"
Things you should never do around a girl you like. [pause] Listen.
Number one. Talking badly about yourself. It's a turn-off, because it signals a lack of confidence and self-esteem.
Number two. Staring at her, or making no eye contact at all. Staring like a deer in headlights is odd. Looking at the ground the whole time is too. Keep your eyes up.
Number three. Being pushy. Show interest, yes. But if she's not giving it back, don't push.
Number four. Putting her on a pedestal. That means you idealise her so much you stop seeing a real person.
[lower voice] Save this for the next time you see her.

## 05 · Dark psychology #41 · "The slow look"
Here's a good flirting tip. [pause] Listen.
On a date, when she's telling you something she really cares about and her eyes light up, watch her as she talks.
You're listening. You hear every word. But part of you is clearly just noticing her.
So instead of jumping in with "yeah, yeah, wow, interesting," say nothing.
Let her notice you noticing her. Slow down, and really hold eye contact while she speaks.
That can be a real form of flirting. It just needs you to slow down and be present.
[lower voice] Save this for your next date.

## 07 · Dark psychology #43 · "Make her miss you"
Let's be clear. She needs to miss you to feel that pull again. [pause] Listen.
If you're always there, always replying, always available, she never gets to feel what life is like without you.
So flip it. Pull back and give her space, and she starts to feel the gap where you used to be.
Absence makes the heart grow fonder, but only if she has time to miss you.
When she pulls away, don't panic. Give her the space.
At first she might enjoy the distance and think she has the upper hand. Then the silence creeps in. What's he up to? Why isn't he reaching out?
When she starts to wonder, you've won. Now she's the one chasing.
[lower voice] Save this for the next time she goes quiet.
