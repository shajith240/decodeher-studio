# Week 2 scripts (draft for owner approval, 2026-10-14)

Voice: plain talk, a guy to his friend. Hook names his exact situation (≤ 12 words). Then a real story, a turn around 12 s, one plain WHY, one move he can copy, a motivational close, and "Follow for more." as its own line.
Sources: the vault briefs in drafts/week2-briefs-a.md and drafts/week2-briefs-b.md. Each script only says what the vault supports. No manipulation: we respect a no, and we never tell him to play games.
Order: 2 a day (08:30 + 21:00 IST), with topics mixed so no two neighbours feel alike.

---
## 01 · Jealousy · "Doesn't move me"
She mentions some guy who texted her… and watches your face.
I used to fail this every single time.
I'd go quiet, ask who he is, then start telling her why I'm better.
And she'd just smile.
Here's what was actually happening.
She wasn't trying to hurt me. She was checking if I'd shake.
And I shook. Every time.
Now I just go, "Nice, he's got taste," and change the topic.
No questions. No speech.
Stop competing with guys you can't even see. Work on you.
Follow for more.

## 02 · Focus · "One second"
You opened your phone for "one second"… an hour ago.
Bro, that was my every day.
Sit down to work, check one notification, then reels, then music.
Night comes, nothing's done, and I feel behind.
Here's what nobody tells you.
Every time you check your phone, your focus breaks. And it takes time to come back.
So try this tomorrow. Phone in another room. One task. Twenty-five minutes.
Then five minutes off. No screen.
First day is hard. By the end of the week, you'll feel it.
You don't need more time, boy. You need your focus back.
Follow for more.

## 03 · Boundaries · "Your plans matter"
You dropped your plans for her again, didn't you?
Yeah. That was me last month.
I was out with my boys. She texted, "come pick me up?" I left in five minutes.
She didn't even say thanks.
Here's the thing nobody tells you.
When you drop everything every time, your time stops meaning anything.
Not to her. Not even to you.
So next time it's not an emergency, just say, "Can't tonight, I'm with my boys. Tomorrow?"
Friendly. No long story.
Your plans matter, bro. Start acting like it.
Follow for more.

## 04 · Provider vs lover · "Not a free service"
Nice guys don't finish last. Always-available guys do.
I was the most reliable guy she knew.
Help with her assignment? Me. Ride at night? Me. Bad day? She called me.
Then she started dating a guy who replies whenever he feels like it.
I was so confused.
But it makes sense now.
I only showed her one side. Safe. Always there.
So she put me in the safe box. Friend.
Stay reliable. Just stop being instant. Keep your own plans.
Be a good man, not a free service.
Follow for more.

## 05 · Frame control · "You almost had me"
She called you boring. And then you started defending yourself.
I literally did this. On a date.
She said, "you're kind of boring," and I started talking about my gym, my job, my trips.
The more I talked, the more bored she looked.
Took me a while to get it.
She wasn't judging me. She was poking to see if I'd panic.
And defending myself was the panic.
Now I just smile and go, "That's cute. You almost had me." Then I talk about something else.
Stop explaining yourself to people. Your vibe says enough.
Follow for more.

## 06 · Saying no · "Fake yes"
You said yes to something you hated. Again.
Bro, I did this all the time.
Party I didn't want. Trip I couldn't afford. Favour I had no time for.
I'd go, sit there annoyed, then explain later why it was hard for me.
Nobody respected me more for it. Not one person.
Because people can feel a fake yes.
So next time, just say, "Nah, I'm not up for that." And stop talking.
No excuses. No long reason.
It feels weird the first time. Then it feels like freedom.
Your time is yours, man. Protect it.
Follow for more.

## 07 · Good-morning guy · "Not every second"
Stop sending her "good morning" every single day. Seriously.
I was that guy. Good morning, good night, every day.
Replied in seconds. Said yes to everything.
She was sweet with me. But she stopped starting conversations.
And when I asked her out, she was busy.
Here's the part that hurts.
When you're always there, she never has to wonder about you.
And when there's nothing to wonder about, the excitement fades.
So live your life for a few days. Let her miss a message.
Be there for her, bro. Just not every second.
Follow for more.

## 08 · Agreeing with everything · "Be someone"
Agreeing with everything she says is why she's bored of you.
That was my whole plan, man. Just agree.
She likes a movie? Me too. She hates something? Me too.
I thought that's how you make someone like you.
But she started treating me like her best friend. Nothing more.
Here's why.
When you agree with everything, there's nobody there. Just an echo.
She can't feel you. So she can't want you.
Next time you don't agree, say it. "Nah, that movie was bad." And smile.
Have your own opinions, man. That's what makes you someone.
Follow for more.

## 09 · "Not tonight" · "Respect her no"
She's flirting all night, then says "not tonight." Here's what that means.
Most guys get this so wrong.
I used to take it personally, or start explaining myself.
Which made it weird for both of us.
But here's what's really going on.
She's not rejecting you. She's protecting herself.
She doesn't want to feel judged. Not by people, not by you.
So when she slows down, just say, "No problem. I like hanging out with you." And mean it.
No pressure. No jokes about it.
Respect her no. That's what a real man does.
Follow for more.

## 10 · Interview texting · "Take it offline"
If your chats die after "what are you up to?", listen.
Bro, my chats used to die like that every day.
"What are you up to?" "Nothing." "What music do you like?" "Everything."
It felt like a job interview.
And I was waiting by my phone for every reply.
Here's why it dies.
Questions like that feel like work. She has to carry the whole chat.
So stop asking. Say something about her instead.
Like, "You're the type who cries at sad songs and denies it."
Then ask her out. Real life is where it counts.
Follow for more.

## 11 · Self-respect · "40 minutes late"
She's forty minutes late, and you said "no problem"?
That was me. Every time.
She'd come late, I'd smile, then thank her for coming.
She cancelled? "No worries." She snapped at me? I said sorry.
I thought I was being nice.
But here's what it was telling her.
That treating me badly costs nothing.
So next time, say it once. "Hey, I waited forty minutes. Not cool." Calm. Not angry. Just clear.
If you don't respect your own time, nobody will. Start today, boy.
Follow for more.

## 12 · Commitment · "Keep yourself"
You finally got her… then you dropped your whole life?
Yeah. I did that too.
Stopped the gym. Stopped seeing my friends. Texted her all day.
I thought that's what a good boyfriend does.
Three months later, she was picking fights over small things.
And I said sorry for everything.
Here's what I didn't see.
She fell for the guy with his own life. Then that guy disappeared.
So keep one thing that's just yours. Gym, friends, your goals.
Don't lose yourself to keep her. Keep yourself. That's who she chose.
Follow for more.

## 13 · Part 2 of reel one · "The one text"
She said she's busy. Again. Here's the one text to send.
Okay, this is part two.
Last time I told you, ask once, with a real day.
So you did. And she still dodged.
Now you send one text. "No worries. If you want to hang out, let me know."
That's it. Then stop.
No memes at 1 a.m. No checking her story.
If she wants you, she knows exactly where you are.
And if she doesn't, you just saved yourself months.
Your energy is for people who show up.
Follow for more.

## 14 · Noise · "Get your mind back"
Be honest. Can you sit ten minutes without your phone?
I couldn't. Not even five.
Earphones on the bus. Earphones at the gym. Reels before sleep.
Then I'd wonder why studying felt impossible.
Here's what I figured out.
When everything is loud all day, normal life starts feeling boring.
So your brain keeps begging for more noise.
Try this. Thirty minutes a day. No phone, no music. Just you.
Walk. Read. Think. It's uncomfortable at first. Then your head gets clear.
Get your mind back, boy. Everything gets easier after that.
Follow for more.

# Replacements (owner 10-14: #02, #06, #14 were off-niche — the channel is women/dating only)

## 15 · Compliments · "Notice dont flatter"
You tell her she's beautiful every time. That's the problem.
I did this for months, man.
Every date, "you look so beautiful." Then I'd wait for her face to light up.
She'd just say "thanks" and change the topic.
Here's what I didn't get.
Anyone can say she's beautiful. She hears it every day.
It doesn't tell her you actually see her.
So say something only you would notice. "That laugh you do when you're nervous? It's cute."
Say it once. Then move on. Don't wait for a reaction.
Stop flattering her, bro. Start noticing her.
Follow for more.

## 16 · Mixed signals · "Just ask"
Flirty on Friday. One-word replies on Sunday. Sound familiar?
Bro, this used to drive me crazy.
I'd read the chat ten times. Write five replies. Delete all of them.
Trying to figure out what I did wrong.
Here's what finally worked.
Most mixed signals aren't a game. They're just things nobody said out loud.
So I asked her once. "You're warm one day, quiet the next. I'd rather ask than guess. Where are you at?"
Calm. No drama. And whatever she says, you accept it.
A man asks. A boy guesses. Be the man.
Follow for more.

## 17 · Being asked to change · "Stay you"
She said "cut your hair," and you did it the same week?
Yeah. I did way worse.
New haircut. New clothes. Stopped seeing friends she didn't like.
I thought she'd like me more. She liked me less.
Here's why. Every time you change on command, she learns you can be moved.
And it's hard to respect someone who keeps moving.
So next time, stay calm. "I'm keeping it for now. What bothers you about it?"
Then actually listen. Real problems, like hygiene, you fix.
She liked you for who you were. Don't trade him in.
Follow for more.
