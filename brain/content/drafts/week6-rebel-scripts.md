## 01 · Dark psychology #1 · "Hot and cold"
One day she can't stop texting you. [pause] The next day, she's gone. [lower voice] Listen carefully.
Psychology calls this intermittent reinforcement, and it's the same trick a slot machine uses.
When the reward comes at random, your brain stops thinking and starts chasing.
So every slow reply feels like a loss, and every sweet text feels like you just won.
That's why you can't stop thinking about her. [pause] It's not love. It's a loop.
[confident] Here's how you break it.
Number one. Don't celebrate the highs. A sweet text gets a normal, warm reply. Not ten messages back.
Number two. Match her pace. If she takes six hours, you don't answer in six seconds.
Number three. Spend your time with people who show up the same way every day.
The moment you stop chasing, the loop loses its grip, and she notices the change.
[lower voice] In the next part, I'll show you what she does when the loop breaks. Follow, so you don't miss it.
