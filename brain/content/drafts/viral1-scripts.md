## 01 · Dark psychology #34 · "Habits women find attractive"
Four masculine habits women find attractive, and none of them cost money. [pause] Listen.
Number one. You move your body every day. Even a long walk counts. Discipline shows before you say a word.
Number two. You eat like you respect yourself. How you treat your body tells her how you treat your life.
Number three. Old-fashioned manners. Hold the door, put the phone away, make sure she gets home safe. Most men stopped, so you stand out.
Number four. You lead. You pick the place, you make the plan, and you stay calm when something goes wrong.
[confident] Psychologists call this competence. We're drawn to people who clearly have their own life handled.

## 02 · Dark psychology #35 · "Six words"
If she says these six words a certain way, she likes you. [pause] Listen. Hi. Yeah. Really. Stop. No. Bye.
One study found that women speak in a higher voice to men they find attractive. The pitch goes up and the tone gets softer.
With a friend, those words come out flat and quick.
With someone she likes, the same words come out slower, higher, almost sing-song, and they turn playful and teasing.
[confident] Here's your move. Don't count the words. Listen to how she says them, and compare it with how she talks to everyone else.
If it's playful only with you, that's your sign to ask her out.
[lower voice] Send this to the friend who can't tell if she likes him.

## 03 · Dark psychology #36 · "Biggest turn-off"
The biggest turn-off in dating should be this, and most men treat it like a challenge. [pause] Listen. Hot and cold. Flaky plans. One-word texts.
Psychologists call it intermittent reinforcement. When attention comes at random, your brain chases it harder, like a slot machine.
So it feels like attraction. It isn't. It's uncertainty.
[confident] Here's the shift.
Number one. Stop reading mixed effort as mystery. Read it as information.
Number two. Notice who lights up when your name pops up, and who makes weekend plans with you.
Number three. If you're always working to keep her attention, step back. That's not a relationship. It's a job.
Someone who's excited about you will show it.
[lower voice] Save this for the next time she goes cold.
