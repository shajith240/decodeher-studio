## 01 · Dark psychology #37 · "Likes you but hides it"
Signs a girl likes you but is trying not to show it. [pause] Listen.
Number one. She remembers small things. Something you said once, weeks ago, comes back in her questions.
Number two. Her eyes keep finding you. She holds your gaze, or looks away fast when you catch her.
Number three. She closes the distance. She sits a little nearer and leans in when you talk.
Number four. She teases you. Light, playful jabs are how many people test if the vibe is mutual.
[confident] One sign means nothing. Three of them, again and again, is a pattern.
[lower voice] Send this to the friend who never notices.

## 02 · Dark psychology #38 · "What women wish men knew"
Two things women wish men knew. [pause] Listen.
Number one. Going sexual too soon kills it. Early on, she's deciding if you're safe to be around. Rushing that answers no.
Number two. Games attract the wrong women. The three-day rule, the fake busy act. The woman who stays for that game wants the game, not you.
[confident] So here's the move. If the date was good, text her that night. "I had a great time. Let's do it again."
Clear interest isn't needy. It's rare.
[lower voice] Save this before your next date.

## 03 · Dark psychology #39 · "Small things she notices"
Small things men do that women find attractive, and most men never notice. [pause] Listen.
Number one. You drive calmly. She wants to feel safe with you, and the way you drive shows it in the first five minutes.
Number two. You fix things. A loose shelf, a laptop problem, a noisy alarm at one a.m. Quiet competence is attractive.
Number three. You're kind to kids and dogs when you think nobody's watching.
[confident] Psychologists call these honest signals. They're hard to fake, so she trusts them more than anything you say.
[lower voice] Send this to the friend who thinks it's all about looks.

## 04 · Dark psychology #40 · "Never do this around her"
Never do these around a girl you like. [pause] Listen.
Number one. Don't put yourself down. A joke about yourself is fine. Constant self-criticism tells her you don't rate yourself.
Number two. Don't stare, and don't avoid her eyes. Look, smile, look away, look back. That's normal.
Number three. Don't push. Show interest once, clearly. If she doesn't return it, let it go.
Number four. Don't put her on a pedestal. If she can do no wrong, you're dating a picture, not a person.
[confident] Every one of these comes down to the same thing. Treat yourself as an equal.
[lower voice] Save this for the next time you see her.

## 05 · Dark psychology #41 · "The slow look"
The most attractive thing you can do on a date costs nothing. [pause] Listen.
When she talks about something she loves and her eyes light up, stop filling the gaps. No "yeah, yeah, wow, interesting."
Go quiet. Hold eye contact a little longer. Let her see that you're listening, and that you're also noticing her.
[confident] She'll feel it. She'll often slow down, smile, and lose her train of thought.
That's not a trick. It's being fully present, and almost nobody is anymore.
[lower voice] Save this for your next date.

## 06 · Dark psychology #42 · "What you lead with"
Whatever you lead with is what she'll stay for. [pause] Listen.
If you won her with money, with the rented car, the photo next to a jet that isn't yours, the dates you couldn't afford, don't be surprised when money is what she wants.
It's simple. What you put on display is what you get chosen for, and it's what she'll keep expecting.
[confident] So lead with what you want to be loved for. Your humour. Your plans. How you treat people.
The right person stays for that.
[lower voice] Send this to the friend who keeps paying to be liked.
