## 01 · Dark psychology #43 · "Make her miss you"
Want her to miss you? Stop being so available. [pause] Listen.
If you answer every text in seconds and you're always free, she never gets the chance to miss you.
Psychologists call it the scarcity principle. We want more of what we can't have all the time.
Number one. Stop replying instantly. Live your day, then answer.
Number two. Fill your week. Gym, friends, work, plans she isn't part of.
Number three. When she pulls back, don't chase. Give her the space.
[confident] The silence does the work. She starts to wonder what you're up to.
And when she reaches out, you're warm, not waiting.
[lower voice] Save this for the next time she goes quiet.
