## 01 · Dark psychology #14 · "Every story, no day"
She watches every story you post, but when you ask to meet, she never gives you a day. [pause]
But watching a story costs her two seconds and nothing else.
In psychology there's an idea called costly signalling. A signal means more when it costs something.
A like costs nothing. Her time costs something.
Real interest looks like effort. She asks you questions, she picks a day, and she shows up.
Here's what to do instead. Stop reading her likes, and make one clear plan.
Text her, "I'd like to see you. Are you free Thursday or Saturday?"
If she picks a day, great. If she says "maybe" and keeps watching your stories, that's your answer.
Being noticed is not the same as being chosen.
[lower voice] Send this to the friend who's decoding every like.
