How to ask a girl out over text? Say the day and the place.
A vague 'what are you up to?' makes her guess what you want. Ask it clearly instead: 'I'd like to take you for coffee on Saturday. Are you free?' Then watch whether she offers another day. That's how to ask a girl out for a date without pressure.
