Hot and cold isn't attraction. It's the biggest turn-off in dating.
Random attention makes your brain chase harder. Psychologists call it intermittent reinforcement, the same pull as a slot machine. Read mixed effort as information, notice who is excited to see you, and step back when keeping her attention feels like a job.
