# Carousel rules (created 2026-10-27)
- Our 5 carousels reached 6-25 people each (performance.md, 10-19 to 10-27); reels reach 110-1,300. Reels are the growth engine, carousels do not bring new people. ([[carousel-reach-check]])
- Buffer study (4M posts): reels +36% reach, carousels +12% engagement. Use a carousel only as a save-worthy recap of an already posted reel, never in place of a reel slot.
- Shape: slide 1 = the same hook as the reel; one point per slide; last slide = the closing line (send or save). Keyword phrase in caption line 1. No texting words.
- Owner-shared carousel tips get folded in here after fact-check.
