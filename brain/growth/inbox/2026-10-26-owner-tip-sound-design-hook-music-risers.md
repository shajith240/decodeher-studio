**Takeaway:** Sound helps only a little. Reach comes from the hook and watch time, so the one cheap thing to test is a single sound hit in the first second. Skip risers and corporate-music worries.

## Claims checked
- ❓ **unproven**: Corporate or stock music "screams unoriginal" and hurts performance — This is a taste claim. Meta says nothing about music genre and ranks on watch time, sends per reach and likes per reach. The only sourced audio statement I found says there is no penalty for popular audio, and that came from secondary blog write-ups, not Meta's own pages, so it is weak. Music choice matters only through how it changes retention. (https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/trending-audio-instagram-reach/)
- ⚠️ **partly**: Impact sounds stop people from scrolling past your video — A sharp sound at second 0 to 1 can lift the 3-second hold, and Mosseri says the first 3 seconds are the hook. But no Meta or major-vendor data isolates sound effects, and many viewers watch with sound off, so the visual and text hook must carry the opening on its own. (https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-guide/)
- ⚠️ **partly**: Risers build anticipation so people watch to the end — Tension build is a real editing technique, and watch time is the top ranking signal. But risers only work for a payoff that is a visual reveal or a beat drop. In a calm talking reel, retention comes from open loops in the script, not from a sound. I found no data showing risers raise completion. (https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-guide/)
- ✅ **works**: The goal is to get people to watch to the end (watch time is what matters) — Mosseri and Instagram name average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the key reel signals. Our own data matches: every reel with watch of 34% or more and 13 s or more average watch reached 920+, and the rest stayed at 118 to 350. (https://creators.instagram.com/)
- ❌ **myth**: "90% of people" make this mistake, so these three changes are stupidly simple fixes — The 90% figure is invented, and presenting three audio tweaks as the main fix for reach is creator-style overselling. Reach is driven mainly by hook, relevance and watch time.
- ✅ **works**: Free sound-effect websites exist for impact sounds — True in general, though licences vary. We don't need this because the owner already supplies a licensed pack.
