# Monetization plan for @thedecodeher (written 2026-10-18)

Honest status: ~31 followers, reach 116-1,119 per reel. **Nothing here is an income promise.** Money only follows reach; the 1,000-followers push comes first. This plan is about being ready, cheaply, so the first sales and deals are not delayed.

## 2. Product
**Recommended first product: "The Decode Her Texting Playbook" - a 20-30 page PDF.**
- Content: the reels' best material made practical: 25 real "she said X, here is what it means and what to send" examples, a what-not-to-send list, 30 honest conversation starters. It is the same ethics as the page (decode, be honest, never manipulate).
- Price: **$7-9** (about Rs 600-750). Impulse price; a US young man pays this without thinking. Test $9 first, drop to $7 if zero sales after 300 bio clicks. A later bundle (+ conversation-starter pack + reply templates) at $15-19 only after a few sales and reviews.
- Make a free lead version first (5-page "5 texts she never explains" PDF) only if we later get an email tool; not needed now.
- Build cost: one Claude session plus design in Canva/our own HTML-to-PDF. No ads, no face.
- Notion template / mini-course: not now. Courses need trust we do not have at 31 followers.

### Conversion reality (assumptions, not data)
- Vendor blogs claim bio-link click to purchase of roughly **1-5%** (one says 2-3% for bio links; none is a measured study). We assume **1-2%** because we have no face, no proof, no reviews.
- Typical funnel: reach -> profile visit (a few % of reach) -> bio click (a fraction of visits) -> sale. Our own measurement matters more than any blog.
- Worked example (assumptions): 100,000 reel views/month, 0.5% click the bio = 500 clicks, 1.5% buy = ~7 sales x $9 = ~$63 before fees. At current ~120-1,100 views per reel the honest expectation is **zero to a handful of sales a month**.
- Realistic first-sale timing: one blog says affiliate/digital products can work "from 1,000 followers" (unverified creator hype). Our view: expect the first sale only once a reel gets 5k+ views with a clear CTA, and plan for months, not days.
- Strongest conversion lever we have: a reel whose closing line points to the exact situation the PDF solves ("I put 25 of these in a PDF, link in bio"), plus a pinned comment. DM-based selling (comment a keyword, send the link) is reported to convert far better than bio clicks (one vendor claims 12-25%; vendor claim, VERIFY by testing).

### Tax and rules for a student (brief; CHECK WITH A CA)
- Foreign income is still taxable income in India; a student with small amounts still files. Ask a CA whether it counts as business income and which ITR form. Keep every Gumroad payout statement.
- GST: guides say registration is needed above Rs 20 lakh/year turnover (Rs 10 lakh in special-category states), but exports of services can be zero-rated under an LUT. Whether a Gumroad (MoR) sale counts as the owner's export is a CA question. Do not register or file anything without a CA answer.
- Foreign payments into an Indian account: banks may ask for purpose codes; a CA/bank can advise on FIRC/eFIRA.
- If the owner is under 18, a guardian-run account may be needed (check the owner's age and platform terms).

## 5. Brand deals
Realism: **not before ~5-10k followers with steady views, and faceless dating advice is a narrow niche.**
- Rates (vendor blogs, directional): nano (<10k) about $25-150 per static post, $50-300 per Reel (Influee 2026; other blogs $50-250). Micro 10-100k: Reels $750-3,000 in the same vendor's guide, which looks high for faceless pages. One unsourced blog says Indian rates run 60-70% below US. Our audience is mostly US/English countries, which is a pricing advantage if Insights confirm it. No dating-niche or faceless-specific benchmark was found.
- Rule of thumb for quoting: price on **expected views**, not followers. Start near $5-15 per 1,000 guaranteed average views for a dedicated reel, and accept lower for the first 2 deals to earn proof.
- Fit brands: men's grooming/fragrance, apparel, dating or conversation apps, self-improvement/books apps, VPN/utility apps, coaching. Dating apps and anything involving alcohol, adult themes or "pickup" claims carry Meta restrictions or reputational risk.
- Where to find: (1) Instagram Creator Marketplace (if shown), (2) inbound DMs, (3) UGC marketplaces (e.g. Collabstr, Insense, Influencer.in, Winkl, Plixxo; I did not verify each platform's India support or fees), (4) direct outreach to small brands.
- What brands check: audience country split (our US share is the selling point), average reel views, 3-second/watch-time, shares/saves, engagement rate, content fit and clean past posts.
- UGC alternative: a few brands pay $150-500 per video with no follower minimum (one blog). Our clip-plus-voice format is not classic UGC, so treat as a test.
- Red flags and scams: asks for money/"verification" fee up front; "send your login/password or code"; product-only "deals" demanding a sponsored post with no fee; unrealistic payment from unknown dating or crypto/forex/betting brands; link to a phishing "brand portal"; requests to pay a "security deposit"; pressure to move off Instagram immediately; no company website, ABN/registration, or invoice terms. Get a written agreement: deliverables, usage rights, payment date, payment before or on posting.

## 6. Affiliate
- Amazon Associates (India and US are separate programs): usable via a link in bio or story link; links must be Amazon-generated tagged links, not copied browser URLs; disclose clearly; Associates rules prohibit affiliate links in emails/newsletters and violation risks account closure (guides from Jan and Jul 2026, not Amazon's own text; **read the current Operating Agreement**). An Indian Associates account earns on India store sales only; buyers from the US need a US Associates account, which needs its own eligibility and US-oriented payout/tax forms (VERIFY).
- Fit: 2-3 books on communication or attraction (non-manipulative) and the occasional grooming basic. Keep it honest; do not link junk.
- How to use faceless: one "Recommended" link in bio page only after the product exists; mention it in a reel only where it fits, with a pinned comment and a spoken "affiliate link" disclosure.
- App referral programs: only for apps we would actually recommend. Affiliate income at our size will be cents to tens of dollars.

## 7. Policy and platform risk
- Branded content: anything of value from a brand (money or free product) needs the **Paid partnership label**, enabled under the Branded content settings in the app; tag the brand, wait for approval (Instagram Help Center; creators.instagram.com/earn-money/branded-content). Some products (drugs, weapons) are banned and some (alcohol) restricted.
- Affiliate and product-link posts should state the relationship clearly (FTC-style disclosure for US viewers; ASCI guidance in India).
- Reach/monetization risk: Instagram's recommendation guidelines and Partner Monetization Policies apply; my search found nothing specific on dating-advice content. Plausible sensitive areas (inference, not verified): manipulation/coercion framing, sexual content, harassment, misleading claims. Keeping the page's rule - decode, defend, honest attraction, no "make her insecure" - lowers this risk. Check Account Status in Instagram once a week.
- Never promise results ("she will text back"). Frame the PDF as examples and ideas, not guarantees; no medical or therapy claims.

## 8. Phased plan
### NOW (31 followers): prep, no selling pressure
1. Owner screenshots Professional Dashboard -> Monetization tools; confirm what is offered and account age eligibility.
2. Create Gumroad account, add Indian bank, check payout minimum/threshold shown in settings (VERIFY $100 vs $10). Create the draft product (unpublished).
3. Claude writes the Texting Playbook PDF (reuse approved reel scripts) and shows the owner the design before any use (project rule).
4. Check Insights for audience country split; save a screenshot monthly.
5. Ask a CA one short question set (income, GST/export, whether the owner files).
Expected income: nothing. Cost: time only.

### AT ~1,000 followers
1. Publish the PDF at $9, link in bio (one link). Pin one reel that solves the same problem.
2. Test CTAs: 1 reel in 6 ends "I put 25 of these texts in a PDF, link in bio"; others keep the send/save experiment. Do not lead every reel with a pitch.
3. Measure: bio taps, Gumroad page views, sales. Do nothing else for 30 days.
4. Add 2-3 honest affiliate links on the bio page only if the owner approves.
Expected (assumptions above): 0-5 sales per month at current growth; a 3-figure dollar month needs roughly 100k monthly views.

### AT ~10,000 followers
1. Request/check Creator Marketplace and gifts/subscription eligibility again.
2. Pitch 5-10 relevant small brands with the template; publish a simple rate card; start with 1-2 discounted deals for proof.
3. Add a $15-19 bundle (playbook + starters + replies) or a small paid community only if buyers ask for more.
4. Consider an email list (free lead PDF) so buyers are not lost to the algorithm.
Expected: first brand deals plausible, still uncertain; no income guarantee.

## 9. What NOT to do
- Do not buy followers, join pods, or use bots (also against owner rules).
- Do not sell a "course" or $50+ product at this size.
- Do not make income or dating-result claims, or fake screenshots/testimonials.
- Do not coach manipulation, gaslighting or "make her chase you" for sales; it violates owner ethics and raises policy risk.
- Do not put anything visual (cover, product mockup, CTA outro) on a live post before the owner sees it.
- Do not pay anyone for "verification", "partnership access" or "monetization unlock"; do not share login or codes with brands.
- Do not file GST or tax forms without a CA.
- Do not rely on one platform; export the buyer list and the PDF source.
