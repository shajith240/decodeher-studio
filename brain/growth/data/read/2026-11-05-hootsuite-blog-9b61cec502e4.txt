Instagram’s AI disclosure policy matters because it affects how people see your content and, in some cases, whether they find it at all. Getting the labels right can also help you keep your audience’s trust. Here’s where it makes the biggest difference: It maintains audience trust People want to know whether what they’re seeing on Instagram is real. For brands, labeling AI content is a simple way to be transparent and protect the trust you’ve worked hard to build. “When audiences find out after the fact that something was AI-generated, they start questioning the whole company, not just that one post,” says Ambreen Sharif , Creative Director of Ambreen Sharif Creative . And as AI gets harder to spot, being upfront can actually work in your favor. “When people can’t tell what’s real anymore, honesty becomes part of the brand itself,” Sharif says. “ Disclosing AI use signals that you respect your audience enough to let them decide how they feel about it, and that kind of respect builds loyalty. ” Your discovery depends on it Recommendations are how Instagram shows your content to people who don’t already follow you. For AI-generated profiles, a missing label can cut off that pipeline entirely. That’s a big deal if your brand relies on Explore to reach people who don’t follow you yet. It keeps influencer partnerships transparent Plenty of brands rely on influencers to make content. But if a creator uses AI and skips a required label, it’s still your product (and your brand’s reputation ) on the line. That’s why brands need to pay attention to AI disclosures even when they’re not the ones hitting “publish.” It reduces compliance risk For some brands, missing an AI label is more than a trust issue. Sharif points to regulated spaces like financial services, where “getting this wrong can also create compliance exposure on top of the reputational hit.” The FTC, for example, bans fake reviews and testimonials that claim to come from people who don’t exist, including ones made with AI. What content do you need to label as AI on Instagram? Instagram requires you to label organic posts that contain realistic-looking videos or realistic-sounding audio that were created or altered with AI. Accounts featuring an AI-generated person also need a separate profile label. Note: AI-generated images don’t require a label, but Instagram may add one automatically. Content that needs a label vs. content that does not Here’s a quick look at what Instagram expects you to label (and what it doesn’t): Content type Needs a label? AI-generated videos that look real Yes Real videos modified with AI (e.g., swapped scenery or added people) Yes Songs or audio with AI-generated vocals Yes Reels with realistic AI voiceovers Yes AI-written captions or scripts No AI-generated or AI-edited images No (but Instagram may add a label itself) Pro tip 💡: Posting to TikTok too? Its rules work differently, so check out our TikTok AI disclosure guide . How do you label AI content on Instagram? To label AI content on Instagram, turn on the “Add AI label” toggle before you share a post, Reel, or Story. If your account features an AI-generated person, you’ll also need to turn on “AI-generated profile” in your profile settings. For ads, Meta will add a label automatically when it detects AI. The exception is ads about social issues, elections, or politics. For those, you’ll need to disclose AI when you set them up. How do you add an AI label to an Instagram post? For a post or Reel: Upload your post or Reel in the Instagram app, then tap Next . Find Add AI label and toggle it on. Hit Share. For a Story: Share a photo or video to your Instagram story. Tap More , then Label AI. Toggle on Add AI label . Hit Share . How do you add the AI-generated profile label on Instagram? If you’re running an account for a virtual influencer or another AI-made person: Go to your Instagram profile. Tap Edit profile . Toggle on AI-generated profile . Once it’s on, the label appears on your profile, next to your content (Feeds, Reels, and Stories), and next to your comments and messages. And if you’re running an AI-generated profile, leaving off the profile label could limit your reach. Here’s what that looks like in practice: Instagram might label a post for you . Meta looks for signs of AI-generated content, so skipping the toggle doesn’t guarantee your post will stay unlabeled. Instagram might penalize you. Meta warns that skipping a required AI label may lead to penalties, but doesn’t specify what those are. Your AI profile might lose reach. When Instagram detects an unlabeled AI profile, it stops recommending the profile to new people. You’ll get a notification if this happens. From there, you can add the missing label or appeal. Bottom line: It’s easier to add the right label before publishing than to deal with the consequences afterward. How can your team manage AI disclosures on Instagram? Your team can manage AI disclosures on Instagram by knowing where AI shows up in your content and checking for it before anything goes live. That includes your own posts and the content influencers create for you. Here’s how, step by step: Get clear on how your team uses AI Check whether you need the profile label Include AI in your influencer briefs Add an AI check to approvals 1. Get clear on how your team uses AI Start by mapping out where AI actually shows up in your Instagram content . Maybe your team uses AI to write captions , generate product images, or add voiceovers to Reels. Only some of those need a label (in this case, just the voiceover). But it’s much easier to make that call when everyone knows which tools are in play. 2. Check whether you need the profile label Do a one-time review of your Instagram account. Do you use a virtual influencer or spokesperson as the face of your brand? If so, you’ll need to turn on “AI-generated profile.” Most brands won’t need this label at all. But it’s worth five minutes to confirm, since an unlabeled AI profile can lose its spot in recommendations. 3. Include AI in your influencer briefs Influencers have their own workflows (and their own favorite AI tools ). So don’t assume you’ll know when they use AI. Set expectations upfront in your creator brief. Outline which kinds of AI content need a label on Instagram, and ask them to flag any realistic AI video or audio before the post goes live. Psst: Instagram’s AI labels aren’t a substitute for disclosing a paid partnership. FAQ: Instagram AI disclosure policy What is Instagram’s AI disclosure policy? Instagram requires you to disclose realistic-looking video or realistic-sounding audio that’s been created or altered using artificial intelligence. That includes manipulated media such as AI-generated people, cloned voices, and realistic AI voiceovers. Do I have to label AI-generated captions or edited photos? No. Using an AI prompt to write your Instagram caption doesn’t trigger the platform’s AI labeling requirement. The same goes for still photos edited with AI tools. Keep in mind that Instagram may still add an “AI info” label to a photo if it detects signs of AI editing. Does Instagram penalize AI-generated content? No, Instagram doesn’t penalize content just because it was made with AI. But you could face penalties for breaking its disclosure rules, like posting realistic AI-generated audio made with voice cloning without adding an AI label. Meta warns that failing to meet its disclosure requirements may lead to penalties, but doesn’t specify what those penalties are. Do you have to disclose AI-generated images on Instagram? No, Instagram doesn’t require you to label AI-generated still images in organic posts. However, Meta may automatically add an “AI info” label if its systems detect signs of AI, like C2PA metadata that many AI tools attach to their files. So even if you don’t add a platform label yourself, your audience may still see one. How does Hootsuite help manage AI disclosures on Instagram?
