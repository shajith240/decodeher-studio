Instagram Plus costs $3.99 a month. Meta started rolling it out globally in June 2026, after testing it in a few markets earlier in the year. Here’s what you need to know before signing up: Price: $3.99 per month. Pricing may vary by country. Availability: Rolling out globally. To see if it’s available where you live, check your Instagram settings. Features: Vary by market. Instagram shows you exactly what features are included during signup. Meta says it plans to keep adding features, and it’s already expanded Instagram Plus since its initial launch with new upgrades, like custom DM and Story fonts. What features does Instagram Plus include? Instagram Plus includes a mix of Story tools , viewer insights, and profile customizations. Story Spotlight and Extend The Story Spotlight is a feature that moves one of your Stories to the front of your friends’ Story trays, so it’s one of the first things they see. Story Extend, meanwhile, keeps a Story live for 48 hours instead of the usual 24. Both features give your Story more visibility when you really want people to see it. Multiple Story Audiences Multiple Story audiences let you create custom lists of people and choose which list sees each Story. It’s similar to Close Friends, but you’re not limited to one list. You can make as many audience groups as you want, like family, coworkers, or close friends. Then you pick which group sees each Story. Story Preview The most elusive feature on this list, Story Preview lets you watch someone’s Story without showing up in their viewer list. Story Rewatch Insights Story Rewatch Count tells you how many times your Story was replayed, while Search Viewer List lets you quickly check whether a specific person saw it. Profile customizations With Plus, you can change the font in your bio and pin up to six posts to the top of your profile. Post directly to profile Normally, a new post shows up in your followers’ feeds. Plus lets you post straight to your profile or Highlights without it appearing in anyone’s feed. It’s a nice option for adding to your grid quietly without making a big announcement about it. Is Instagram Plus available for Professional or Business accounts? No, Business accounts generally aren’t eligible to sign up. Instagram Plus is mainly designed for personal use. Almost every Plus feature is about friends, like who sees your Stories and how your profile looks. It doesn’t come with ad tools, extra analytics, or anything that could meaningfully help your brand account. If you’re looking for more business-forward perks, Meta One creator and business plans might be a better fit. They include verification and impersonation protection, plus growth tools on the higher tiers. FAQ: Instagram Plus What is Instagram Plus? Instagram Plus is a premium subscription that unlocks extra Instagram features for individual users. It’s focused mostly on Stories, profile customization, and how you interact with friends. The regular version of Instagram is still free. Plus is an optional upgrade for people who want the exclusive features. Meta currently positions it as an individual subscription, not a business tool. How much does Instagram Plus cost? Instagram Plus costs $3.99 per month. Pricing can vary by country, so check the price shown in your Instagram account before signing up. Does Instagram Plus remove ads? No. Instagram Plus adds features, but you’ll still see ads in your feed, Stories, and Reels. Can people tell if you viewed their Story with Story Preview? No. When you use Story Preview, you won’t show up in that person’s viewer list, so they won’t see that you watched their Story. What does Instagram Plus include? Instagram Plus includes extra Story controls, viewer insights, and profile customization tweaks. That includes 48-hour Stories, multiple Story audience lists, Story rewatch counts, custom app icons and fonts, and the ability to post directly to your profile without pushing the post into your friends’ feeds. Instagram notes that Plus is still evolving, with new features added over time. Is Instagram Plus worth it? Instagram Plus may be worth it if you’re an everyday user who wants more control over Stories, your profile, and how you share with friends. It’s less compelling for brands. That’s because you don’t get extra analytics, ad tools, or other business-focused perks from Instagram Plus. Meta offers separate Instagram subscriptions for creators and businesses through Meta One, which launched in September 2026. How do you subscribe to Instagram Plus? To subscribe to Instagram Plus, check the subscription options available in your Instagram settings and follow the signup prompts if Plus appears for your account.
