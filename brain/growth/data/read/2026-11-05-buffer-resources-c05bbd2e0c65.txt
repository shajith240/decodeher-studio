Start your story Click + New Post > Post in the top right of your Buffer dashboard to open the composer. Choose Instagram from the Channels list on the top left. Choose Story as the post type. Step 2. Add your photo or video Drag and drop your image or video into the composer — or click to browse your folders. Check the preview on the right to see how the story will look once it's live. ⚡ Pro tip: Each photo or video goes out as its own story, so if you've got a sequence planned, schedule each one separately. Step 3. Make quick edits Notice a photo needs a little tweak? No problem. Just hover over the image and click the pencil icon to edit. You'll be able to crop, rotate, blur, brighten, and more — handy for last-minute touch-ups. ⚡ Pro tip: Not quite ready to publish? Save it as a draft and come back to it later. (Here's a guide to using drafts in Buffer .) Step 4. Set the time your story goes live Pick Automatic (Buffer posts it for you) or Notify Me (Buffer sends a reminder to your phone and you finish the post in Instagram). Choose when it goes out: Set Date and Time to pick a specific day and time — Buffer also suggests the times your audience is most likely to be active Next Available to drop it into your next open posting slot Prioritize to bump it to the top of your queue (paid plans) Now to post it straight away Click Schedule Post . 🎶 Want to add links, music, or text? Choose the Notify Me option and toggle on Music, Text, or Link. You can drop the actual text or link, or a note about the song you plan to use, in the fields that open up. These will appear in the Buffer mobile app when the time comes to post. Business, creator, or personal account: what publishes automatically How Buffer publishes your story depends on your Instagram account type: Business and creator accounts: Buffer can publish your story automatically at the time you choose. Personal accounts: Buffer sends a notification to the Buffer mobile app when it's time to post. You tap it and share the story from Instagram. Even on a business or creator account, a story switches to a phone reminder if it includes stickers, a link, text, music, or a caption, or if your image or video falls outside Instagram's accepted size. Buffer shows you which method a story will use before you schedule it. (Our help center has the full rundown of automatic vs. notification publishing .) And that's it — your story is scheduled. 🎲 Now that you're on a roll, here are some tried-and-tested ways to get more followers on Instagram . How to schedule a story on Instagram from your phone You can do all of the above from the Buffer app on iOS or Android: Tap the green + icon at the bottom right to open the composer. Tap the channel icon and select your Instagram account. Tap Story . Upload your photo or video. Tap Automatic or Notify Me . Pick a scheduling option — and you're done! If you choose Notify Me — or your story needs a sticker or link — the Buffer app pings you at the scheduled time, and you finish the story in Instagram. You'll need push notifications turned on for that to work. (Here are the mobile scheduling steps in our help center , with screenshots.) How to schedule a story with Meta Business Suite Meta Business Suite is Meta's free tool for managing Facebook and Instagram, and it can schedule Instagram Stories from both your desktop browser and the Meta Business Suite app. In your browser: Navigate to business.facebook.com to access Meta Business Suite, and log into the correct account. Click the Create Story button. Select your Facebook and/or Instagram accounts. Upload media (images and videos) for your story. Click Edit to add text, mentions, or stickers. To schedule the Instagram Story to publish later, click the Schedule button. 🌿 While you're batching stories, you might as well line up your feed posts too. Here's how to schedule Instagram posts , step by step. With the Meta Business Suite app: Tap Content from the bottom navigation bar (or tap the + / Story button on the home screen). Select Story and add your photos or videos. Choose Schedule for later to set your date and time. Tap Schedule . What happens to stickers, polls, and links when you schedule a story? Instagram doesn't let third-party tools add interactive elements to a story automatically. That covers link stickers, polls, questions, music, and story text, plus captions on stories. So if a story needs any of these, it can't publish on its own — even on a business or creator account. The workaround is simple. In Buffer, add the sticker you want (or a note about it) and the story switches to Notify Me . Buffer leaves a placeholder for each sticker so you remember what to add, sends the story to your phone at the scheduled time, and you add the sticker in Instagram before you share it. Scheduling your stories keeps you posting consistently, and consistent stories keep your existing followers engaged — which is what the Instagram algorithm wants to see. Stories might not be the best format for reaching new followers, but the more someone watches or interacts with your stories, the more likely Instagram is to show them your future posts and reels. Here’s Adam Mosseri, the Head of Instagram , on how stories factor into the Instagram algorithm. “The most important signals we look at when ranking Stories are the likelihood that you’ll tap on the story, like the story, or reply with a message,” he says. Scheduling takes the pressure off needing to be "on" all the time, so your stories keep showing up even on your busiest days. Here's what that looks like in practice: Stay consistent without burning out: Scheduling ahead helps you keep showing up without scrambling for content, and without feeling like you have to be glued to your phone 24/7. Post with more intention: I've definitely posted a "just so I've posted something" story. Scheduling breaks that habit, because you're choosing what to share a few days out, not five minutes before bed. That extra time makes it easier to tie each story back to what you're working toward — a launch, a promo, a newsletter signup. Plan for key engagement moments: Tease a new product drop, share a promo code, or ask a poll question (finished in Instagram), so you're showing up at the top of your followers' feeds when it counts. Batch your stories in one sitting: Ever spent way too long hunting down screenshots and videos scattered across your phone, laptop, and maybe even an old Google Drive? I've been there. Pull everything together once, schedule it, and get on with the rest of your to-do list. Post when your followers are online: You might not always be online when your audience is, but scheduled stories can be. With scheduling, you can post your stories when your followers are most active without setting a reminder or stopping what you're doing. Plan from your desktop (or your phone): Sometimes, creating content on a bigger screen just feels better. Personally, I also prefer to keep my laptop for work, and my phone for personal stuff. Instagram Story scheduling FAQs Can you schedule a story in the Instagram app itself? No. Instagram's built-in scheduler covers Reels, photos, and carousel posts, but not stories (as of November 2026). Instagram hasn't added stories to it, so scheduling a story needs Meta Business Suite or a third-party scheduler that connects to Instagram, like Buffer. Can you schedule Instagram Stories for free? Yes. Meta Business Suite is free and schedules Instagram Stories from desktop or its mobile app. Buffer's free plan also includes Instagram Story scheduling for up to three channels, from desktop or mobile, with up to 10 scheduled posts per channel at a time. Can you set a time for a story on Instagram? Not in the Instagram app. With a scheduler, you pick the exact date and time. In Buffer, choose Set Date and Time when you schedule your story, and Buffer will also suggest times when your audience is most likely to be active. Can you schedule Instagram Stories from your phone? Yes, with the Buffer app for iOS or Android: tap Publish , open the composer, choose your Instagram account, tap Story , upload your media, and pick a time. You can also schedule stories in the Meta Business Suite app. Can you schedule Instagram Stories on a personal account? Yes, with Buffer, but they won't publish automatically. Buffer sends a reminder to your phone at the scheduled time and you finish the story in Instagram. To have stories publish on their own, switch to a free business or creator account. Meta Business Suite works with professional accounts only. Will a scheduled story post automatically? It depends. With Buffer, stories on business and creator accounts publish automatically, as long as they don't include stickers, links, text, music, or a caption. Stories on personal accounts, or stories with those features, are sent to your phone to finish in Instagram. What's the best way to schedule Instagram Stories for the week? Batch them. Set aside one session to gather your photos and videos, schedule each story into its slot, and use Buffer's calendar view as your Instagram Stories calendar so you can see the whole week at a glance. Setting a weekly posting goal (and keeping your streak going ) helps you stick to it. How do you edit or delete a scheduled Instagram Story? In Buffer, head to the Queue or Drafts tab for your Instagram channel. Click on the scheduled Instagram Story you want to change. From there, you can edit the content, update the timing, or delete it entirely before it goes live.
