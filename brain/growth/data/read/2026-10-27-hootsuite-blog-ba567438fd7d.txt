The newest Instagram features focus on video editing, profile control, and resharing other people’s content. Here’s what’s landed most recently, and why each one matters for marketing teams. For ongoing changes, we track every release in our Instagram updates hub . The Edits video editing app Longer Reels Customizable profile grids Native reposting Instagram Plus subscription Links on posts Music on carousels and profiles Instagram Edits app Instagram Edits is a standalone video editing app Instagram launched in 2025. It includes a project timeline, a teleprompter for scripted pieces, audio and beat alignment tools, and performance insights for the videos you export. For marketers, the appeal is practical: you can take a Reel from idea to finished edit without buying a separate editing tool, then publish straight to Instagram. Longer Reels Instagram has extended how long a Reel can run, giving brands room for tutorials, product walkthroughs, and short interviews that previously had to be chopped into parts. Longer doesn’t automatically mean better. Use the extra runway only when the story needs it, and watch your retention curve to see where people drop off. Customizable profile grids You can now rearrange the posts on your profile grid instead of being stuck with reverse-chronological order. The grid also displays in a taller, vertical format. This turns your profile into something closer to a landing page . Pin your best-performing or most on-message posts to the top three rows, since that’s what a new visitor sees before deciding whether to follow. Native reposting Instagram added an official repost button, so you can share someone else’s public Reel or feed post to your own audience with attribution built in. Reposts appear in a dedicated tab on your profile rather than mixing into your main grid. It’s a clean way to amplify user-generated content and partner posts without screenshots or third-party repost apps. Instagram Plus subscription Instagram Plus is a paid subscription tier that bundles extra customization, early access to features, and additional insights. It’s aimed at heavy users and creators rather than large brand teams. If you manage a creator-led account, it’s worth a test. If you’re running a multi-account brand presence, your analytics and workflow needs will outgrow it quickly. Links on posts Instagram has opened up link placement beyond the bio, including the ability to add links to Reels and posts for eligible accounts. Availability still varies by account type and region. Check whether your account has it before you build a campaign around it, and keep a bio link as the fallback destination. Music on carousels and profiles You can add music to carousel posts the same way you add it to Reels, and you can pin a 30-second track to your profile. To add profile music, tap Edit Profile, hit Add Music, search for a song, pick your clip, and tap Done. Map search Instagram’s long had location capabilities, but it’s improved its map search features. Users can explore popular tagged locations around them, making it easier to find businesses, mural photo ops, and other noteworthy public places. You can filter location results by specific categories like restaurants, cafes, or salons. What Instagram AI features should marketers know about? It’s useful for getting past a blank screen or generating a few variations to choose between. Treat the output as a first draft. AI-generated Story backgrounds Instagram’s restyle tools can generate or restyle a Story background from a text prompt, which saves design time on quick, low-stakes Stories. It’s a good fit for polls, questions, and announcements. For anything that needs to look on-brand, stick with your own templates. Voice translations and captions Meta AI can translate Reels audio into other languages with lip-synced dubbing, and Instagram adds automatic captions to video. Both reduce the manual work involved in reaching audiences in more than one market. Always review translated audio before publishing, especially for product names and claims that don’t translate cleanly. AI-assisted ad tools On the paid side, Meta’s Advantage+ tools use AI to handle creative variations, placements, and budget allocation across Instagram and Facebook. You set the objective and guardrails, and the system tests combinations for you. The tradeoff is less granular control, so keep a clean measurement setup to confirm what’s actually driving results. What hidden Instagram features should marketers know? These Instagram hidden features aren’t flashy, but they quietly save social teams a lot of time. Hide, delete, or filter comments on your posts You have control over who interacts with your content on Instagram. This is crucial, especially for business accounts. The last thing you want is a runaway comments section full of negativity. To filter comments by keyword: Go to Settings and tap Privacy , then Hidden Words. Toggle Hide comments to filter general offensive words. Click Manage custom words and phrases for messages, comments, and posts to add custom filters. How to delete comments: Tap the speech bubble icon below the comment you want to delete. Swipe to the left over the comment text. Select the garbage can icon that appears to delete the comment. You can also delete your own comments using the same method. To disable comments entirely: You can’t turn off comments for your entire profile, only for individual posts. While posting an image, tap Advanced Settings at the bottom. Toggle on Turn Off Commenting to disable comments for that post. Create quick reply responses to common messages If you run a business account on Instagram, you might suffer from receiving the same messages over and over again. You can streamline responding to frequently asked questions and comments on Instagram Business accounts with saved replies . Here’s how: Go to Business in your Settings, then Quick Replies. Alternatively, click the three-dot chat bubble icon at the bottom of your screen and select New Quick Reply. Create a shortcut for a longer response you often send. For example, use “returnpolicy” as a shortcut for a longer response about your return policy. Enter the longer response associated with the shortcut. When you want to use the response, either type the shortcut into the comment box or tap the Quick Replies icon to choose from your saved responses. Favorites feed You can create a favorites list to see more of the accounts you care about most. Their posts appear higher in your Feed, and you can switch to a favorites-only Feed to check in on them. This Favorites Feed comes without ads or suggested posts and is totally anonymous. To select your favorites, tap the Instagram logo at the top left corner and add your preferred accounts to the list. Social teams can use it as a lightweight way to monitor competitors and partner brands. Reset your content recommendations Instagram lets users reset the recommendations driving their Explore page and Reels feed, clearing the slate on what the algorithm thinks they want. It’s worth knowing because it’s a reminder that audience interest signals are not permanent. Treat Explore reach as something you earn repeatedly, not a position you hold. Schedule Stories and messages Instagram now supports native scheduling for feed posts, Reels, Stories, and DMs. You’ll find it under Advanced Settings in the composer, or in the chat window for messages. Native scheduling is fine for a single account. If you’re managing several, you’ll want approvals and a shared calendar, which the app doesn’t offer. What is the 3-second rule on Instagram? The 3-second rule on Instagram refers to the idea that you have roughly three seconds to hold a viewer’s attention before they scroll, which makes your opening frame and hook the single most important part of a Reel. Does Instagram tell you if someone keeps looking at your profile? No, Instagram does not tell you if someone keeps looking at your profile. There’s no native feature that identifies or counts individual profile visitors, and any app claiming otherwise isn’t using official Instagram data. What is the Instagram Edits app? The Instagram Edits app is a standalone video editing tool from Instagram that includes a project timeline, teleprompter, audio and beat alignment, AI-assisted effects, and performance insights for the videos you publish. What is Instagram Plus? Instagram Plus is a paid subscription tier that gives users extra profile customization, additional insights, and early access to some features beyond what free accounts get. How do Instagram Broadcast Channels work? Instagram Broadcast Channels work as one-to-many message feeds. Followers opt in, only the channel owner and invited guests can post, and updates land in the audience’s DM inbox. Can you schedule posts natively on Instagram? Yes, you can schedule posts natively on Instagram. The app supports scheduling for feed posts, Reels, Stories, and direct messages, though it doesn’t include approvals or multi-account calendars. What Instagram features are only available for business accounts? Instagram features limited to business accounts include the Professional Dashboard, Instagram Insights, contact buttons, product tags, ad creation, quick replies, and competitive insights. How do Instagram collab posts work? Instagram collab posts work by letting multiple accounts co-author the same post or Reel. You can invite up to five collaborators, so the content can appear across six profiles in total, and likes, comments, and views are shared across all accounts involved.
