# send-or-save-closing-line
Status: testing (see experiments/2026-10-16-closing-line-send-vs-save.md)
- What: the last spoken line gives one concrete reason to SEND the reel to a friend or SAVE it for later. Instagram ranks reels on sends per reach and watch time (Mosseri, official; the "3-5x" multiplier is a third-party estimate).
- Why: all 4 posted reels have 0 shares and 0 saves per 1k, while likes are 8-54 per 1k. People like it, but nothing makes them pass it on or keep it.
- Our pipeline: the closing line in each script and the last line of the caption. Natural and specific, never begging, never "follow for more".
- Evidence: Mosseri ranking inputs (official, see expert-sources.md); our own zeros (small sample, 4 reels).
