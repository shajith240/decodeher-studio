# send-or-save-closing-line
Status: testing (see experiments/2026-10-16-closing-line-send-vs-save.md)
- What: the last spoken line gives one concrete reason to SEND the reel to a friend or SAVE it for later. Instagram ranks reels on sends per reach and watch time (Mosseri, official; the "3-5x" multiplier is a third-party estimate).
- Why: the first 4 posted reels had 0 shares and 0 saves per 1k while likes were 8-54 per 1k. People liked, but nothing made them pass it on or keep it.
- Our pipeline: the closing line in each script and the last line of the caption. Natural and specific, never begging, never "follow for more".
- 10-17: the 3 new-format reels showed shares 0-14 and saves 0-24 per 1k at 110-208 reach. Also [[2026-10-17-hootsuite-engagement-rates-2026]] (shares/saves outrank likes).
- 10-19: still no w8 reel posted, no verdict. The nine posted reels (w2) had mixed closings. Shares were 8-77/1k on the four big reels, so reach, not the closing line, explains most shares so far. The owner tip confirms sends per reach is the signal ([[2026-10-19-owner-tip-damini-algorithm-signals]]).
- Evidence: Mosseri ranking inputs (official, see expert-sources.md); our own data (small sample).
