Honest summary: only username/name matching is first-party proven. Everything else is cheap and harmless, so do it, but do not expect SEO to replace reach from the Reels feed (watch time, sends).

## Rules for our factory
1. Caption line 1 = the exact search phrase as a plain sentence + verdict (already the rule). Pick the phrase from [[keywords]]; use the man's wording ("she", "girl", "texting"), not psychology jargon.
2. Same phrase in three places per reel: caption line 1, title card (3-7 words, caps), first spoken sentence (within the first 3 s; also lands in auto-captions). Do not stuff more than one primary phrase.
3. Caption line 2-3: one long-tail variant naturally ("...over text", "...after a first date"). 2 sentences total of body.
4. Hashtags: 3 (max 5). `#datingadviceformen` + 2 topic tags that equal the phrase (#mixedsignals, #textingtips). Drop #darkpsychology/#psychologyfacts from reels about a concrete behaviour; they are broad and off the real search intent. Tags are context, not a reach lever.
5. Do not put keyword lists in the first comment or alt text. No stuffing.
6. Profile: Name field carries the keyword (see owner proposals in the report / below). Pin the top reels (needs owner's hands).
7. Make sure the account is Professional (Creator) and public, and the Google-search toggle is on.
8. Measure: after 10 reels with the new line-1 rule, compare Insights "reach by source: Explore/Search" vs older. n small: descriptive only.
