# viewer-action-hook
Status: testing (default for the next 6 reels, owner order 10-19). Not a clean test: the next batch has no her-behaviour reels to compare with.
- What: the first sentence names the viewer's own move or mistake plus tension ("Stop doing X after she Y"); her behaviour comes second.
- Our data (10-19, 9 reels, small): the two clearest viewer hooks (favour, chasing) reached 1,143 and 1,108, watch 46% and 35%. But two her-behaviour hooks also broke out: "small test" 1,238 and "three hours, then a meme" 920 (34%). The five weak reels (118-272) are all her-behaviour. So viewer hooks 2 of 2 big, her-behaviour 2 of 7 big. A hint, not proof; topic, slot and music also differ.
- What lines up best with reach: watch % ≥ 34 and avg watch ≥ 13 s (all 4 big reels). Holding people is the common factor, whoever the subject is.
- Pipeline: script hook + title card. Keep her exact behaviour inside the line when it fits.
- To judge: compare watch % and reach by hook type once more reels post (≥ 3 each). Needs the retention drop point per reel ([[top-reel-pattern-review]]).
