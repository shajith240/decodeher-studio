# audition-first-batch
Status: idea (explains our data; nothing to change by itself)
What: Instagram shows a new post to a small test group of non-followers, then keeps pushing it only if it does well (Mosseri, [[2026-10-27-buffer-instagram-algorithm-2026-guide]]).
Our data (10-27): 25 reels landed at about 110-270 reach (15 reels) or about 1,000-1,300 (10 reels). Nothing between, no reel above 1,300. Looks like "failed the audition" vs "passed one expansion step".
Pipeline map: the first 3 s (AI opener, title card, hook line) and the first 24 h decide. Judge a reel by whether it passes the audition, not by raw reach.
Evidence: official Meta via Buffer. Experiments: none. Open question: what lifts the pass rate (avg watch 16 s or more: 7 of 9 passed; below 16 s: 3 of 16 passed).
