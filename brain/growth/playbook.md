# Playbook (updated 2026-10-26; section F numbers are from 10-19, no newer data)
Only official guidance, our own proven numbers, and the owner's fixed rules are rules. Everything else is "testing" or "idea".

## A. Ranked rules (official or proven)
1. The first 1-3 s decide the reel: readable with sound off, opening on the AI-woman clip. Aim for skip rate under ~40% (vendor rule of thumb; check Reel Insights retention chart) (Mosseri: first 3 s are the hook).
2. Reels are ranked on watch time, sends per reach and likes per reach (Mosseri). Finish strong, give a reason to send. Shares and saves outrank likes ([[2026-10-17-hootsuite-engagement-rates-2026]], [[2026-10-19-owner-tip-damini-algorithm-signals]]).
3. Hold people: in our data every reel with watch >= 34% and avg watch >= 13 s reached 920+; the rest stayed at 118-350. Never rush the voice, cut dead seconds.
4. Post steadily, 3 a day (08:30, 18:30, 22:00 IST). Judge formats only on 6+ reels; one reel is noise.
5. Sharp video, no blur or grain. Original content only, no reposts or watermarks.
6. Keyword phrase in caption line 1, title card and first sentence ([[keyword-seo]]). Answer comments (automation does it).

## B. Owner's fixed rules (always win)
- Format: hook (her behaviour + tension) > "Listen" > named psychology > numbered moves > why > natural closing line. English, calm voice (Fish 86ac), never rushed, no length cap. Templates T0-T10 in brain/rules/script-writing-system.md.
- Look: colour clips, lens frame, small white serif captions, title card with no box. Opening AI-woman clip. No clips with baked-in text.
- Sound: only the owner's rebel-pack SFX, rare snaps; only the 10 approved piano/horror tracks, bed 12 dB under voice. NO trending audio.
- Words: no texting words nobody says aloud (haha, lol, lmao, omg, hehe, idk, tbh) in a script, and no reel built around one.
- Ethics: decode, defend, honest attraction. Never make a woman insecure or dependent. No bots, pods, follow-for-follow, bait.
- Show before you ship: nothing new visual goes on a live post unseen. No generated covers.
- Owner 10-19: every new script's first sentence names the viewer's own move or mistake + tension; her behaviour second.
