# Playbook (updated 2026-10-27; numbers from performance.md of 10-27: 25 reels, 5 carousels, 74 followers)
Only official guidance, our own proven numbers, and the owner's fixed rules are rules. Everything else is "testing" or "idea".

## A. Ranked rules (official or proven)
1. The first 1-3 s decide the reel: readable with sound off, opening on the AI-woman clip. Instagram "auditions" each post on a small group of non-followers and keeps pushing it only if it does well ([[audition-first-batch]], Mosseri via Buffer). Our reach split matches: 15 reels stopped at 110-270, 10 passed to 1,000-1,300, none in between.
2. Reels are ranked on watch time, sends per reach and likes per reach (Mosseri). Sends matter most for reaching non-followers, so give a reason to send. Shares and saves outrank likes ([[2026-10-27-buffer-instagram-algorithm-2026-guide]]).
3. Hold people: reels with average watch of 16 s or more passed the audition 7 of 9 times; under 16 s only 3 of 16 did (our data, small). The old "13 s" line was too low: three reels with 13.4-13.8 s stayed at 115-132. Never rush the voice, cut dead seconds.
4. Post steadily, 3 a day (08:30, 18:30, 22:00 IST). The first 24 h matter most. Judge formats only on 6+ reels.
5. Sharp video, no blur or grain. Original content only: reword every line, own voice, own visuals ([[original-content-check]]).
6. Keyword phrase in caption line 1, title card and first sentence ([[keyword-seo]]). Answer comments (automation does it).
7. Reels bring new people; carousels do not (our 5 carousels reached 6-25; Buffer: reels +36% reach). See carousel-rules.md.

## B. Owner's fixed rules (always win)
- Format: hook (viewer's move + tension) > "Listen" > named psychology > numbered moves > why > natural closing line. English, calm voice (Fish 86ac), never rushed, no length cap. Templates T0-T10 in brain/rules/script-writing-system.md. 95/5 remix of a proven post; narration editor runs before voice.
- Look: colour clips, lens frame, small white serif captions, title card with no box. Opening AI-woman clip. No clips with baked-in text.
- Sound: only the owner's rebel-pack SFX, rare snaps; only the 10 approved piano/horror tracks, bed 12 dB under voice. NO trending audio.
- Words: no texting words nobody says aloud (haha, lol, lmao, omg, hehe, idk, tbh) in a script, and no reel built around one.
- Ethics: decode, defend, honest attraction. Never make a woman insecure or dependent. No bots, pods, follow-for-follow, bait.
- Show before you ship: nothing new visual goes on a live post unseen. No generated covers.
