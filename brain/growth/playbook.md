# Playbook (updated 2026-10-16)
Only official guidance, our own proven numbers, and the owner's fixed rules are rules. Everything else is "testing" or "idea".

## A. Ranked rules (official or proven)
1. The first 1-3 s decide the reel: relatable behaviour of hers, readable with sound off, opening on the AI-woman clip. (Mosseri: first 3 s are the hook; vendor data: 43-67% swipe by 3 s.)
2. Reels are ranked on watch time, sends per reach and likes per reach (Mosseri). So: finish strong, give a reason to send.
3. Post steadily: 3 a day at 08:30, 14:00, 21:00 IST. Judge formats only on 6+ reels; one reel is noise.
4. Sharp video, no blur or grain (Instagram guidance). Original content only, no reposts or watermarks.
5. Answer comments (automation does it).

## B. Owner's fixed rules (always win)
- Format: hook (her behaviour + tension) > "Listen" > named psychology > numbered moves > why > natural closing line. English, calm voice (Fish 86ac), never rushed, no length cap.
- Look: colour clips, lens frame, small white serif captions, title card with no box. Opening AI-woman clip. No clips with baked-in text.
- Sound: only the owner's rebel-pack SFX, rare snaps; only the 10 approved piano/horror tracks, bed 12 dB under voice. So NO trending audio.
- Ethics: decode, defend, honest attraction. Never make a woman insecure or dependent. No bots, pods, follow-for-follow, bait.
- Show before you ship: nothing new visual goes on a live post unseen. No generated covers.
