# Playbook (updated 2026-10-31; performance.md: 103 followers; reels 45-47 still show 0 stats = fetch gap)
Only official guidance, our own proven numbers, and the owner's fixed rules are rules. Everything else is "testing" or "idea".

## A. Ranked rules (official or proven)
1. The first 1-3 s decide the reel: readable with sound off, opening on the AI-woman clip. Instagram "auditions" each post on a small group of non-followers and keeps pushing it only if it does well ([[audition-first-batch]]). Our reach split matches: 15 reels stopped at 110-270, 10 passed to 1,000-1,300, none in between.
2. Reels are ranked on watch time, completion, sends per reach and likes per reach (Mosseri via Buffer; Hootsuite repeats it). Sends reach non-followers most, so give a reason to send. Shares and saves outrank likes.
3. Hold people: reels with average watch of 16 s or more passed 7 of 9 times; under 16 s only 3 of 16 (our data, small). Since 10-22 scripts grew to 45-60 s and 5 of 6 reels (37-42) stopped at about 120. So: short, 85 words, see [[short-script-85]].
4. Post steadily, 3 a day (08:30, 18:30, 22:00 IST); consistency beats volume (Hootsuite). No reel went out 10-24/25: keep the gap closed.
5. Sharp video, no blur or grain. Original content only: reword every line, own voice, own visuals ([[original-content-check]]).
6. Keyword phrase in caption line 1, title card and first sentence ([[keyword-seo]]). Answer comments (automation does it).
7. Reels bring new people; carousels do not (our 5 carousels reached 6-25; Buffer: reels +36% reach). See carousel-rules.md.

## B. Owner's fixed rules (always win)
- Copy what is viral now (95/5): remix a proven post, same hook, points, order, examples, prefer the last 60 days and our faceless format. Ours: "Listen." beat, closing line, our voice.
- Short: max 85 spoken words, max 3 points, no pause before "Listen.". Hook names her exact behaviour or a type of man. Complete connected sentences, English only.
- Look: colour Pinterest clips, lens frame, small white serif captions, AI-woman opener with no text on her face, bronze-bust outro. No clips with baked-in text.
- Sound: only rebel-pack SFX, only the approved piano/horror tracks, bed 12 dB or more under the voice. NO trending audio.
- Words: no texting words nobody says aloud (haha, lol, lmao, omg, hehe, idk, tbh) in a script, none as a topic.
- Ethics: decode, defend, honest attraction. Never make a woman insecure or dependent. No bots, pods, follow-for-follow, bait.
- Show before you ship: anything new and visual goes to the owner as a preview first.
