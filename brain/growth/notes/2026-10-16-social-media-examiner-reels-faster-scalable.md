# Social Media Examiner: Making Instagram Reels Easier, Faster and Scalable
- Source: Social Media Examiner (Elizabeth Marberry), 2026-10-15
- URL: https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/making-instagram-reels-easier-faster-scalable/

Claims
1. Volume alone ("mid" content) hurts reach; the aim is steady posting plus constant refinement. Evidence: expert experience. [[volume-with-quality]]
2. Judge results only after a big enough sample: people quit after ~20 days of 3 Reels a week. She posted daily for a year with little growth, then gained 120k over the next 12 months. Evidence: one creator's story. [[volume-with-quality]]
3. Grainy or unclear video hurts reach (Instagram has said so for years); film/export at the highest quality. Evidence: expert experience, plus long-standing Instagram guidance. [[sharp-video-quality]]
4. Compare top reels on four things: setting/visuals, on-screen text + hook, edit speed (a cut every ~2 s for B-roll), length. Find the pattern and make more of it. Needs 90 days of data. Evidence: expert method. [[top-reel-pattern-review]]
5. Set one goal per reel (follows, sends, comments); views alone mislead. Evidence: expert opinion. [[top-reel-pattern-review]]
6. First Draft / Edits tools: not useful for our pipeline (we render ourselves). Skipped.
