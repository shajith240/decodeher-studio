# Hootsuite: average engagement rates for 13 industries 2026
Source: Hootsuite Blog, https://blog.hootsuite.com/average-engagement-rate/, published 2026-10-13
- "Good" engagement rate is 1-5%; Instagram average 4.2% across industries. Evidence: vendor benchmark, method unclear. Loose yardstick only. [[top-reel-pattern-review]]
- Engagement counts likes, comments, shares, saves and DMs; likes are the lowest-effort signal, shares and saves the strongest. Evidence: opinion, matches Mosseri guidance. Supports [[send-or-save-closing-line]].
- Small accounts can out-engage big ones because the rate is relative to audience size. Evidence: opinion. Our likes/1k (30-45) look fine; the gap is shares, saves and comments ([[comment-prompt]]).
