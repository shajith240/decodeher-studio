# Hootsuite: TikTok AI disclosure policy
- Source: Hootsuite Blog (vendor quoting TikTok Community Guidelines). URL: https://blog.hootsuite.com/ai-disclosure-policy-for-tiktok/ . Date: 2026-11-03.
1. TikTok requires an AI label on realistic AI-generated people or scenes, and on voice clones. Evidence: platform policy via vendor. Our AI-woman opener is realistic AI video: label it if we ever post on TikTok. [[ai-label-check]]
2. Generic text-to-speech voiceover and AI-written scripts need no label. Evidence: same. Our narration is generic AI voice: fine.
3. Missing label can mean auto-label, reduced reach or removal; the label itself does not cut reach. Evidence: TikTok statement via vendor.
4. Rules differ per platform; check Instagram's own "AI info" setting before relying on this (not verified here). Evidence: vendor opinion. [[ai-label-check]]
