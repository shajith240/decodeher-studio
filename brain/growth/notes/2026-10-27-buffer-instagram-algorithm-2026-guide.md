# Buffer: How the Instagram Algorithm Works, 2026 guide
Source: Buffer Resources (data blog) · https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/ · published 2026-10-23 · read 2026-10-27

- Watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach are the top three signals (Mosseri, quoted). Likes count slightly more for followers, sends slightly more for non-followers. Reels reach mostly non-followers, so sends matter most for us. Evidence: official Meta (Mosseri), second-hand. → [[send-or-save-closing-line]]
- "Auditioning": Instagram shows a small public account's post to a few non-followers first and keeps pushing it only if it does well. Evidence: official Meta (Mosseri). Matches our reach split (about 120 or about 1,100, nothing between). → [[audition-first-batch]]
- Originality: Instagram favours original creators; accounts that mostly repost are not recommended to non-followers (aggregator reach cut since April 2026). Evidence: official Meta. Risk check for our 95/5 remix rule. → [[original-content-check]]
- Recency: more of each day's impressions go to posts under 24 h old, so the first 24 h decide. Evidence: official Meta. → [[audition-first-batch]]
- Advice: open with a clear hook, name the topic in the caption, make it shareable. Evidence: official guidance. → [[keyword-seo]]
- Reels get 36% more reach than carousels; carousels 12% more engagement (Buffer, 4M+ posts). Evidence: data study. → [[carousel-reach-check]]
