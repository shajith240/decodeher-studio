# Hootsuite: followers needed to get paid on Instagram
Source: Hootsuite Blog, https://blog.hootsuite.com/how-many-followers-on-instagram-to-get-paid/, published 2026-10-16
- Gifts need 500 followers (professional account); Subscriptions and badges need 10,000; brand deals and affiliate links have no minimum. Evidence: vendor summary of official thresholds.
- Brands look at engagement, not only follower count. Evidence: opinion.
- Relevance: monetisation comes later. 500 is a useful mid milestone toward 1,000. No tactic for now.
