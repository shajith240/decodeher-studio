# Sprout Social: social media audience segmentation (B2B guide)
- Source: Sprout Social Insights blog. URL: https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-audience-segmentation/ . Date: 2026-10-28.
- Mostly written for brands and teams; little applies to a one-person faceless page. Claims worth keeping:
1. 66% of consumers feel more selective about what they engage with than a year ago; 56% see "AI slop" (generic one-size-fits-all content) often (Sprout Q1 2026 survey, vendor data, unverified). Maps to [[original-content-check]]: reword, own voice, one original example per reel.
2. Platforms increasingly reward relevance over reach; content aimed at a specific group beats generic content (vendor opinion). Maps to hooks that name a type of man or her exact behaviour ([[viewer-action-hook]]).
3. Segment by behaviour and live engagement data, not static demographics (expert opinion). For us: read which hooks and topics earn watch % and saves ([[top-reel-pattern-review]]).
4. Revisit segments at least once a quarter (opinion). Not actionable at 79 followers.
