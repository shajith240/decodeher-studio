- Calls to action should keep viewers inside the video (save / send), not "link in bio". Evidence: expert opinion. Matches our closing line. [[send-or-save-closing-line]]
- Consistency beats volume: a steady weekly cadence gives Instagram more chances to learn who to show it to. Evidence: expert opinion. [[volume-with-quality]]
