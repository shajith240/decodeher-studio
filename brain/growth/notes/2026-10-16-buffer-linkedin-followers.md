# Buffer: How to Increase LinkedIn Followers
- Source: Buffer Resources, 2026-10-05
- URL: https://buffer.com/resources/how-to-increase-linkedin-followers/
- Note: LinkedIn-only article. Only two ideas travel to Instagram.

Claims
1. Replying to comments helps: ~83% of LinkedIn profiles did better when they replied (Buffer data, LinkedIn only). Evidence: data study, other platform. [[reply-to-comments]]
2. Stick to 3-5 topics so the feed can match you to an audience. We already have one niche. Evidence: vendor guidance. No new tactic.
3. Posts flagged "seems like AI slop" lose ~40% of views (LinkedIn, vendor figure, unverified). Supports the owner's "no AI slop" bar. No new tactic.
