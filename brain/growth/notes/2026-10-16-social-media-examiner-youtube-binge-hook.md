# Social Media Examiner: YouTube Retention Strategy (BINGE method)
- Source: Social Media Examiner (Adam Ivy + Michael Stelzner), 2026-10-08
- URL: https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/youtube-retention-strategy-how-to-hook-hold-and-convert-viewers/
- Note: written for long-form YouTube. Only the hook and loop ideas transfer to Reels.

Claims
1. The first words should cause a feeling (fear, anger, FOMO), not announce a topic. Speak to what the viewer feels, not only what they know. Evidence: expert experience. [[feeling-first-hook]]
2. Hook + "intrigue" must land in about 8 s: first the feeling, then a tease of the payoff, with no spoilers. Evidence: expert experience. [[feeling-first-hook]]
3. "Yo-yo" pronoun pattern (you feel this / we're told this / I found out / we can do this / you choose). Evidence: expert experience. [[feeling-first-hook]]
4. Numbered listicles kill retention: "step one, step two" shows how much is left, and an intro that lists every step lets people leave. Fix: each point is useful but signals something is still missing. Evidence: expert opinion, one strategist, no data shown. [[open-loop-body]]
5. Alternate open loop and closed loop between points so there is no natural exit. Evidence: expert opinion. [[open-loop-body]]
