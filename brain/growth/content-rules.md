# Content rules (living rulebook, rewritten daily by the growth session; every script writer follows it)
Updated 2026-10-27. Evidence: owner rules, our 25 posted reels (performance.md 10-27), viral bank (81 YouTube Shorts, 202 Instagram posts, all ≥1M).

## Fixed (owner)
1. Every reel remixes a proven post at 95/5: same hook, same points in the same order, same examples, similar length. Ours: "Listen." after the hook + our closing line. Reword every sentence, never copy word for word. Add one original example or named idea ([[original-content-check]]).
2. Source order: owner shares (owner.jsonl) → viral bank (≥1M, biggest first) → Dark Needle only if the bank is empty.
3. AI-woman opener, zero text on her; captions start after she's gone. Skip skits and street interviews (no narration to keep).
4. Mainstream niche tactics are fine; hard line only at real abuse. Never make her insecure or dependent.
5. 95/5 copies the IDEAS, never the format. Text screenshots become spoken narration with full framed sentences; tools/script_polish.py runs before any voice.
6. No texting words (haha, lol, omg, idk, tbh) in scripts or as topics.

## Learned (our numbers, small samples)
- Reach is two-level: 15 reels at 110-270, 10 at 1,000-1,300, none between ([[audition-first-batch]]). So the goal of every reel is to pass the first audition.
- Hold: avg watch of 16 s or more passed 7 of 9; below 16 s only 3 of 16. Second beat ("Listen" + named idea) before 15 s, no dead lead-in, first move stated before 35 s. Length 39-55 s shows no pattern.
- Hooks: first sentence names the viewer's move or mistake + tension. Winning first lines: "Want her to like you more? Don't do her a favour. Let her do one for you." (1,150), "The more you chase her, the faster she pulls away. It's not bad luck." (1,117), "She teases you, just a little, to see what you'll do." (1,277). Hook-type data stops at 10-18 (small).
- Shares: best 76/1k on "why chasing fails". Closing-line test (send vs save) closed 10-27 as "no signal", so no closing-line rule beyond our fixed line. Running now: [[short-script-85]] (reels 37-42: 1 of 6 passed 1,000; Hootsuite also says drop slow intros and title cards, idea [[first-frame-hook-text]]).
- Sources: no reel is tied to a source with views in performance.md, so no per-source ranking. Do not invent one.
- Owner shares so far: hook > build-up > pay-off (robertmckeaveney); sound hit at second 0-1 is an optional test only ([[opening-sound-hit]]).

## Stop doing
- Topics built on texting words; hooks that only describe her with no viewer move; moves that make her insecure; carousels as a growth tool ([[carousel-reach-check]]); risers and trending audio.

## 10-27 rule: short and no dead air (from our own numbers)
Reels that hold ~17 s average watch reach ~1,100–1,300; reels at 10–13 s die at ~120. Since 10-22 every reel died at ~120, and the
scripts had grown to 45–60 s. Rule: max 85 spoken words (~30 s), max 3 points, "Listen." with no pause, the hook line on screen from
the first frame (above or below her face, never on it).
