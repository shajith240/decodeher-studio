# Content rules (living rulebook — rewritten daily by the growth session; every script writer follows it)
Started 2026-10-19. Evidence so far: owner rule + viral bank (81 YouTube Shorts, 202 Instagram posts, all ≥1M or top-10% outliers).

## Fixed (owner)
1. Every reel remixes a proven post at 95/5: same hook, same points in the same order, same examples, similar length.
   Ours: "Listen." after the hook + our closing line ("Save this…" / "Send this to the friend who…"). Reword, never copy word for word.
2. Source order: what the owner shared (owner.jsonl) → viral bank (≥1M, biggest first) → Dark Needle key ideas only if the bank is empty.
3. AI-woman opener, zero text on her; captions start after she's gone.
4. Mainstream niche tactics are fine; hard line only at real abuse.

## Learned (updated daily)
- Data gap (10-26): performance.md and data/ stop at 10-19 (9 reels, all week-2 format). No bank-sourced reel has numbers yet, so no per-source ranking is possible. Do not invent one.
- Our only evidence (9 reels, small): reels with watch >= 34% and avg watch >= 13 s reached 920-1,512; the other 5 stayed at 118-354. Hold people before 15 s, no dead lead-in.
- Hook subject: viewer-move hooks 2 of 2 big (favour 1,143, chasing 1,108); her-behaviour hooks 2 of 7 big. Hint only.
- Length 39-55 s: no pattern. Closing line: no data yet (send-vs-save test pending). Best shares 77/1k on "why chasing fails".
- Owner share (robertmckeaveney reel): hook > build-up > pay-off, same as our format; keep the answer for the end. No views known, so a structure check, not a source.
- Stop doing: topics built on texting words; hooks that only describe her with no viewer move; moves that make her insecure.
5. (owner 10-26) 95/5 copies the IDEAS, never the format. A text-screenshot or on-screen-list source still becomes spoken narration:
   every quoted text is framed in a full sentence ("If you text her '…', she'll …", "Instead, say '…'"), complete sentences linked
   with so / because / but. tools/script_polish.py (the narration editor) runs on every draft before any voice is recorded.
