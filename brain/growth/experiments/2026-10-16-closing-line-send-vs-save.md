# Experiment: closing line, SEND vs SAVE
- Started: 2026-10-16 (applies to the next 6 scripts, week 8; week 7 closings are already approved, don't touch them)
- Hypothesis: a closing line that names a person to send it to gets more shares per 1k than one that asks for a save.
- The single change: the last spoken sentence (and the caption's last line).
  - Arm A (send): "Send this to the friend who keeps asking why she went quiet."
  - Arm B (save): "Save this for the next time she goes quiet."
  Everything else (hook style, length, clips, music, slots) is the normal approved format.
- Metric: shares per 1k reach (main), saves per 1k reach (second), plus watch %. Read at 48 h after posting.
- Size: 3 reels per arm, and each arm gets one reel in each slot (08:30, 14:00, 21:00) so the slot does not decide the result.
- End: when all 6 reels have 48 h of data (about 2026-10-23 if they post 10-19 to 10-20).
- Warning: reach is about 120 per reel, so one share is 8 per 1k. A difference of 1-2 shares is noise. Only call a winner if one arm has at least 3 more shares in total; otherwise record "no signal" and test the next change.
- Result: pending.
