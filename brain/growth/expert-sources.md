# Expert sources for Reels / short-form growth

Checked 2026-10-16 (curl on every feed; YouTube page scrape for subscriber counts; web search for facts). Audience: the daily growth agent for @thedecodeher.
Rule of thumb for the agent: **official Meta statements > vendor studies with stated sample sizes > creator educators > anything else.** Numbers from vendors are estimates, not Meta data.

Rejected or unverifiable (do not follow): any "10k followers in a day", bot/pod/buy-follower accounts, faceless-growth course sellers (the "Zyon 100k in 7 days" style newsletters and Gumroad "blueprints" turned up in search and are unverified sales pages).

## A. Official Meta / Instagram

### 1. Adam Mosseri (head of Instagram), @mosseri on Instagram and Threads
- Type: official, platform head. URL: https://www.instagram.com/mosseri/ (Threads: https://www.threads.net/@mosseri)
- RSS / channel: none (Instagram and Threads do not publish feeds). Read via the Instagram app or page; weekly "Ask Me Anything" style Q&A videos.
- Learn: his stated Reels ranking inputs (Jan 2025: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach). Trial Reels, "Your Algorithm" topic controls, originality policy (less reach for reposted/watermarked content). Direct word on what is changing.
- Trust evidence: runs Instagram; every product change is announced by him first. Note: the "sends count 3-5x more than likes" multiplier circulating in blogs is a third-party estimate, not a Meta number (checked 2026-10-16; I could not find a primary source for it).
- Frequency: several posts a week. Trust: high.

### 2. Instagram "Ranking explained" (about.instagram.com)
- Type: official explainer. URL: https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained
- Blog feed: https://about.instagram.com/blog/rss returns HTTP 200 but contains 0 items on 2026-10-16, so it is NOT in the JSON list. Read the page as text.
- Learn (fetched 2026-10-16): Reels is a discovery surface, "the majority of what you see is from accounts you don't follow"; ranking predicts likelihood to reshare, watch to the end, like, open the audio page; signals roughly in order: your activity, your history with the poster, reel content (audio, visuals, popularity), poster popularity. Reposted, watermarked, low-resolution, muted, bordered or mostly-text reels get less distribution.
- Trust: first-party. Frequency: updated occasionally. Trust: high.

### 3. Instagram for Creators (@creators) and creators.instagram.com
- Type: official. URL: https://creators.instagram.com/ and https://www.instagram.com/creators/
- RSS: none found (https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rss returns 404).
- Learn: Insights definitions (skip rate, watch time, retention), monetization, product how-tos straight from Meta.
- Frequency: a few posts a week. Trust: high.

## B. Data and research outfits

### 5. Socialinsider (benchmarks)
- URL: https://www.socialinsider.io/instagram-benchmarks (2026 organic benchmarks) ; blog https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/
- RSS: https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/rss/ (200, latest item 2026-10-07)
- Learn: engagement-rate benchmarks by format and follower size, from large sample studies; use them to judge whether our ~0.4-0.5% engagement is normal. Their 2026 page (seen via search 2026-10-16) reports an overall Instagram engagement rate around 0.45% in Q2 2026 and carousels around 0.55%. Secondary sites quote conflicting Reels numbers (0.48%, 0.52%, 1.23%): always read the original page.
- Trust: data company, publishes methodology. Frequency: ~weekly. Trust: high.
