# Pricing and offer: Decode Her: The Texting Playbook (written 2026-10-19)

Question from the owner: "$9 looks cheap. How would Hormozi price and package it?"
Status: recommendation only. Nothing on the live site changes until the owner has seen the new offer block (show before you ship). Site files were not edited.

**Short answer:** the trouble with $9 is that nothing around it explains it, so the price reads as "cheap" instead of "easy yes". Hormozi's rule is to raise the value and the price together, never to drop price against competitors. With ~35 followers, no reviews and cold Reels traffic, the honest version of that is:
- **Two choices on one Polar checkout:** *The Playbook* **$17** and *The Complete Pack* **$27**.
- **A real launch price for the first 100 copies only:** $9 and $19, enforced by a Polar discount with max redemptions = 100. It ends by itself, with no fake countdown.
- **A 30-day no-questions refund.**
- **Three bonuses built from content we already have.**

---

## 1. Hormozi's framework, in our words (secondary sources; I did not read the books themselves)

**Value equation.** How much an offer is worth to a buyer = (the dream outcome × how likely he believes he'll get it) ÷ (how long it takes × how much effort or sacrifice it costs him). There are four levers. Most sellers only push the first one, the dream outcome. The other three are usually cheaper to improve. For us:
- Dream outcome: "know what to send her tonight, and get the date". This is already strong.
- Perceived likelihood: our weakest lever, because we have no reviews and no face. We raise it with the decoder demo on the site, a free sample (/free), a guarantee, and later real buyer quotes.
- Time delay: an instant download, plus a lock-screen cheat sheet that works in 5 seconds.
- Effort: copy-paste replies, so he doesn't have to rewrite anything.

**Grand slam offer.** Don't sell the single item (a PDF). Sell a bundle that solves every problem the buyer hits on the way to the outcome. Hormozi's process: list every obstacle, turn each one into a solution, pick the ones that are cheap to deliver but valuable to him, then wrap them with bonuses, a guarantee, honest scarcity or urgency, and a good name. The goal is an offer that can't be compared line by line with a cheap competitor, which takes you out of the price war.

**Pricing.** "Charge more." A higher price buys:
- margin, to spend on making the product and the marketing better;
- a buyer who takes it more seriously and uses it;
- a signal of quality.

A product priced like a commodity gets judged like a commodity. Hormozi's fix for "too expensive" is to raise the value, not to cut the price.

**Bonuses.** Each bonus should knock out one specific objection or obstacle. It gets its own name and an honest value. It is often presented as more valuable than the core. Bonuses should be things the buyer would actually pay for.

**Guarantees.** The book describes four kinds:
- unconditional: a refund with no questions asked;
- conditional: the buyer must do certain actions first;
- anti-guarantee: "all sales final", with a stated reason;
- implied: performance-based, such as a revenue share.

His claim is that a strong guarantee lifts sales by more than it costs in refunds. That claim comes from his own businesses, not a study.

**Scarcity and urgency.** These raise demand only when they are real: a limited number of spots or copies, a real deadline, or a cohort start. Hormozi says they should be honest. A fake one damages trust once people notice.

**Naming (MAGIC).** Make a magnetic reason why, Announce who it's for, Give a goal, Indicate the time it takes, and Complete it with a container word: playbook, pack, system. Summaries spell the letters slightly differently.

**Low-ticket products ($100M Money Models, 2025).** His model has four parts:
- an attraction offer that brings in first buyers, often discounted;
- an upsell or a menu of options straight after;
- a downsell for people who say no;
- continuity: a subscription or repeat purchases.

The aim is for the first 30 days of a customer's spending to cover what it cost to get him. A cheap front-end product is fine *as long as something comes next*. In tripwire / self-liquidating-offer language this is the $7-47 entry product. Its job is to turn a follower into a buyer, not to earn the profit. Our $9 PDF already is this attraction offer. What's missing is the "what comes next" (a higher option) and anything that makes $9 look deliberate.

**What Hormozi would *not* do here:** a "$997 value" stack we can't defend, fake timers, or competing with free Gumroad PDFs on price.

## 3. Price psychology for cold Instagram traffic (evidence quality noted)

| Idea | Evidence | Strength | What we take from it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Charm prices (ending in 7 or 9) | Anderson & Simester (2003, *Quantitative Marketing and Economics*), a mail-order field test of a dress at $34 / $39 / $44. $39 sold the most (21 vs 16 and 17), and the result held when repeated. The effect was strongest on new items. | Real field study, small numbers | Use $17 / $27 / $9 / $19, not $15 / $25 / $20 |
| Cheap converts better from cold social traffic | Only blogs and anecdotes (an Indie Hackers founder: "$27 bought without much thought"). Neil Patel's "9% under $50" figure has no source. | Weak | Under ~$30 is an impulse buy for a US 20-something. The jump that hurts is past ~$47, not $9 to $17 |
| Anchoring and a menu of options | Hormozi's menu/anchor upsell; well established in behavioural pricing | Medium | Show the $27 Pack next to the $17 Playbook. A higher option makes the lower one look reasonable, and some buyers will pick the Pack |
| Order bumps | Vendor data: SamCart says 30-40% take rate; Focus Digital (2026 report, 1,847 businesses, method not shown) says 37.8%; the usual range quoted is 20-40% | Vendor-biased | **Polar has no order bump**: one product per checkout. So we use "customer picks one of two" on the same checkout link |
| Fake former prices | FTC Guides Against Deceptive Pricing, 16 CFR 233.1: a "was $X" price must be a real price, offered in good faith for a reasonable time | Legal rule | Never "~~$19~~ $9" unless $19 was really charged. Say "launch price, then $17", which is a true statement about the future |

## 4. Recommendation (US buyers, now)

### Offer structure
One Polar checkout link with two products; the buyer picks one. Polar supports this.

| | **The Playbook** | **The Complete Pack** |
|---|---|---|
| Regular price | **$17** | **$27** |
| Launch price (first 100 copies, counted by Polar) | **$9** | **$19** |
| 73-page Texting Playbook (25 situations, never-send list, 30 openers, cheat sheet) | yes | yes |
| Bonus 1: Lock-Screen Cheat Sheets | yes | yes |
| Free updates (new situations added to the PDF) | yes | yes |
| Bonus 2: Her Signals Field Guide | - | yes |
| Bonus 3: The Reply Bank | - | yes |
| 30-day refund | yes | yes |

**How the launch price works:**
- Set it up as a Polar discount, a fixed $8 off each product.
- Max redemptions = 100 in total, preset on the checkout link so it applies automatically.
- When 100 copies are sold, Polar stops applying it and the price becomes $17 / $27 with nothing else to do.
- The site says "Launch price: first 100 copies".
- Optional and honest: a "copies left" number, if we read it from Polar's API. Never a made-up one.

### The 3 bonuses (built only from content we already have; about 1 session each)
1. **Lock-Screen Cheat Sheets.** Three 1170×2532 phone wallpapers:
   - the 3-step method plus the 5 principles;
   - the "quick reads" table;
   - the 12 never-send texts.

   Source: the book's Cheat sheet and Never-send sections. Objection it removes: "I'll forget it when she actually texts." Time to build: about 1 hour with our existing PDF design (book.css).
2. **Her Signals Field Guide.** About 20 extra behaviours *not* in the book's 25: emoji habits, playing hard to get, the talking stage, why girls ghost, "wants to be friends", what she finds attractive, first-text and first-date nerves, and so on.

   Source: the approved reel scripts, weeks 6-11 (36 scripts in brain/content/drafts/week6..11-rebel-scripts.md). Each one gets one page: what it is, what it usually means, the move. Drop any that overlap the book, and ship only distinct ones. The final count may be under 20, and the copy must state the true number.

   Objection it removes: "My situation isn't one of the 25."
3. **The Reply Bank.** All 25 "send this" replies plus the 30 openers, as one copy-paste text file (Notes-friendly) and a one-page index by situation.

   Source: book content, reformatted. We say plainly that the replies are the same ones as in the book, laid out for one-tap copying. Objection it removes: the effort of rewriting replies under pressure.

Plus **free updates**: Polar lets us replace the downloadable file, and buyers re-download from their customer portal. Only promise this if we commit to adding at least one situation a month. Otherwise drop the line.

Not now: a "send us your text, we decode it" DM service. It would be the strongest bonus Hormozi-style, but it costs the owner's time and we have no face or voice to deliver it with. Revisit it as a $47+ tier once there are buyers asking.

### Guarantee
**30 days, unconditional:** "If it doesn't help, email us within 30 days and get every cent back. No questions."

Why this is safe:
- Polar already reserves the right to refund within 60 days at its own discretion to prevent chargebacks, so a "no refunds" stance protects us very little anyway.
- A chargeback costs **$15** each. That is more than a whole $9 sale, so an easy refund is cheaper than a dispute.

Costs:
- The Polar fee is **not** returned on a refund (about $0.95 lost on a $9 sale).
- On refunds, turn **off** "revoke benefits" only if we accept that people keep the file. Recommended: leave revocation on (the default) for one-time purchases.

Before going live:
- Add a short refund line to the site footer or FAQ.
- Add a support email.

### Tradeoffs (honest)
- **Volume beats margin at our size.** With ~35 followers, any price gets 0 to a handful of sales a month. The launch price keeps the entry easy where it matters now, while $17 / $27 shows the real value. The risk is that $17 converts noticeably worse than $9 once the launch copies run out. We'll measure that rather than guess.
- **Looking cheap vs being accessible.** The context makes $9 look deliberate: "launch price", the guarantee, the higher option next to it. It doesn't have to look cheap. Staying at $9 forever with no higher option is what reads as low value and leaves money on the table.
- **Refunds.** Digital, unconditional, and under $30: expect a few percent. Dating-advice buyers who don't get the girl may ask for one. Accept it; it is cheaper than chargebacks.
- **Complexity.** Two products, one discount and three bonuses is about 3 short build sessions. Bonuses 1 and 3 are quick. Bonus 2 is the real work.

### When to raise or change the price
- **The 100 launch copies are gone:** the price becomes $17 / $27 automatically. Announce it a few days before, once, with no countdown theatre.
- **Run a fair test of $17 against $9:** we need at least ~300 checkout-page views per arm before judging. Until then, don't flip-flop.
- **5+ genuine buyer quotes** (asked for by email after purchase, used with permission, never invented): consider $19 / $29 and add the quotes to the site.
- **At about 1k+ followers with steady Reel reach**, add a third option around $47: Pack + something new, such as audio or video breakdowns or a monthly decode drop. That is the "what comes next" in Hormozi's model.
- **If the Pack sells under 15% of orders** after 50 orders, the bonuses aren't pulling. Rework bonus 2 before touching the price.

**The Complete Pack: Launch $19 · then $27** (label: *Most complete*, not "most popular" until it actually is)
- Everything in the Playbook
- **Bonus: Her Signals Field Guide.** [N] more behaviours decoded, from mixed emojis to the talking stage, for when your situation isn't one of the 25.

**Value framing (honest, no inflated "worth $997")**
> One drink on a date costs more than this. One wrong text costs more than that.
> 25 situations for $9 is about 36¢ each.

**Guarantee line**
> Read it for 30 days. If it doesn't help, email us and get every cent back. No questions, no forms.

**Under the buttons**
> Secure checkout by Polar · Instant PDF download · Reads on your phone · Yours to keep

> Nothing in here is a trick. No jealousy games, no silence as punishment. Just reading her honestly and being clear about yourself.

What not to add: invented reviews or "1,000+ men" counts, timers that reset, "only 3 left" unless it's the real Polar count, or "worth $XXX" totals.
