# Decode Her: The Texting Playbook, sales page

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation (owner, 10-18: "be like an expert frontend engineer and build me a good site
that sells the product and maintain the vibe … anyone visit this site should feel the art of design and the craftsmanship").

## The eight topics
1. **Vibe:** a private museum after closing; a leather book on black marble; bronze, candle-gold, crimson-black. References:
   Penguin Classics black covers, a museum wall label, a late-night text thread.
2. **Journey (owner's words, from the chat):** the product must sell; the visitor feels design and craftsmanship.
   Authored sequence: the cover → the problem he knows → try it yourself → what the book holds → why it is honest → buy.
3. **Energy:** quiet open (a title page), tension (the 11 rereads), the loudest moment is interactive (the decoder),
   calm substance (the contents), conviction (the approach), a still close.
4. **Feeling, stage by stage, and the one moment:** see the curve below. Peak: the decoder.
5. **What no other site does:** you tap her actual text and the page reads it for you, margin-note style, then types the reply.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal:** editorial and luxurious, not minimal-SaaS. No eyebrow labels (owner ban), no template look.
7. **One world or scenes:** distinct chapters with hard cuts between dark and ivory grounds, exactly like the book itself.
8. **Assets:** 6 approved art plates (Codex, owner-requested), the book's own pages, the bronze logo. No footage. No reviews yet,
   so none are shown (fake ones are refused).

## Grammar: chaptered editorial
The product is a book; the honest pitch is "read this". Filmic one-shot (no single film), live surface (not software),
continuous world (no geography), typographic poster (we have strong art), gallery (one product, not a range),
split stage (no two-sided argument), rhythmic cutlist (wrong energy for a calm, honest guide) all lost.

## Feeling curve
- Title page: **recognition of quality** (the live typeset cover, gold frame, nothing else).
- The problem: **"that's me"** (she said "we'll see", you reread it eleven times; the bust lit by a phone).
- The decoder (PEAK): **relief, then "oh, that's smart"** (tap her text, the page reads it, the reply types itself).
- The book: **trust in the substance** (25 real situations typeset as a contents page with real page numbers).
- The approach: **respect** (no games; two statues facing each other).
- Close: **calm decision** (a colophon; price, format, delivery; one line to buy).

Peak sentence: "There's a site where you tap the text she sent you and it tells you what it means and what to send back."
Tell-someone: "It's the site where the book reads her text for you before you even buy it."
Authored silence: the full-bleed rose plate before the colophon is deliberate rest, not dead scroll.
